## Own examples

- **AC 5, KvK before the reset** (fails): server `Etc/GMT+2`, KvK on Sunday 12:00 ST (`EVENT_WEEKDAY` 0), now Sun 2027-03-14 03:00 ST, so KvK (14:00 UTC) comes before the reset (Mon 02:00 UTC), and New York changes clocks at 07:00 UTC in between. `America/Bogota` and `America/New_York` split correctly, but the New York row says "Clocks change before the reset" when the change comes before KvK, the first moment after now. With the Saturday schedule the same thing happens for a zone that changes on Saturday between 00:00 and 12:00 ST (e.g. `Pacific/Auckland` on the first Saturday of April, now on Saturday morning ST).
- **AC 3, fixed-offset zones** (passes): `Etc/GMT-1` ×2 with `Asia/Manila` gives rows "UTC+1" 2 and "Manila · UTC+8" 1. `Etc/GMT-1` ×2 with `Africa/Lagos` ×1 gives one row "Lagos · UTC+1" 3.
