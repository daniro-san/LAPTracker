## D132 — The squads read is a daily queue drained by every tick, and every /v1/players answer is kept: the body less the roster for 90 days, the shield and map fields as columns, troops, Hall of Honor and the history rows (2026-11-20, owner, issues 472 and 490; amends D117, D125)

Why: D117 read the squads in two batches of about 100 s at 23:30 and 23:45 server time (ST), with one retry inside the same window. A slow night or an upstream incident left members unread until the next night (owner, 2026-11-19, issue 472). The same call returns much more than squads: the shield (#474), the map scan's time, the player's history, the troops and each hero's Hall of Honor level. D117 dropped all of it (owner, 2026-11-20, issue 490). Raid prep (#474), previous alliances (#461), the Me view (#487), KvK recovery (#489) and Insights need it. Both changes touch the same call, so they are built together.

### The daily queue (issue 472)

- **One queue per alliance and quota day.** `squad_queue` holds one row per member: `(day, union_id, uid)`, `state` (`waiting`, `done`, `failed`), `attempts` and `due_at`. `day` is the daily read of that quota day (`quotaDayRead`). The quota day starts at 00:00 UTC, and with the server at UTC−2 and the daily read at 23:30 ST, Thursday's read (Friday 01:30 UTC) is Friday's quota day. The day's first batch claims the day's `squad_reads` row as D117 does. Every batch then puts the day's roster in the queue (`insert … on conflict do nothing`), due at the row's start, so a roster member always goes before a retry or a newcomer.
- **Small batches on every tick.** Every cron-job.org tick (D30) starts one batch per alliance after its answer (`after`, `scheduleSquadsBatch`). There is no time window any more. A batch reads at most `SQUADS_BATCH_CALLS` (25) members whose `due_at` has come, oldest first, sequential and paced (`PACE_MS`, 500 ms), with a deadline of `SQUADS_BATCH_MS` (60 s) counted from before the claim. The tick's own work takes at most 100 s, so a batch fits in the tick's `maxDuration` of 300 s with room to spare. Each workspace with its own key (D124) runs its batch alongside the others, on its own claim.
- **Nothing on most ticks.** Before the day's read is done (`daily_read_pending`), when no member's turn has come, while the batch waits for the reserve, and once the day is over, a batch makes no call and writes no `squads.batch` line. A batch never starts when it would end after the next 00:00 UTC (`squadsQueueDay`), so every call is counted on the quota day it lands on. What is left at 00:00 UTC is not read: the next quota day has a new queue, made after its own daily read.
- **One answer per member a day** (INVARIANT `squads.once-per-member`). As soon as a call's answer or failure is in, before anything is stored, one transaction writes the day's counts under the batch's claim (`batch_at` equals the time it claimed) and the member's queue row (`saveCall`). A function killed while storing therefore never makes a later batch call that member again. A member with an answer is `done`.
- **Spread-out retries.** A 5xx, no answer, or a call the deadline cut after it was sent moves the member to the back of the queue: `attempts` + 1 and `due_at` 15 minutes later (`SQUADS_RETRY_AFTER_MS`), so it is retried on a later tick. After the third attempt (`SQUADS_ATTEMPTS_MAX`) it is `failed` for the day. Any other failure (a 404, a schema error) is `failed` at once. A call the deadline cut before it was sent is not a call: the member keeps its turn.
- **A 429 or an empty quota pauses the key until the reset.** A 429 stops the day (`quota_429`, D117). On a workspace key, the key is also marked `out_of_quota` (D124), and `squadTargets` skips it until 00:00 UTC. The next quota day starts a new queue. A 401 on a workspace key marks it `invalid` and stops the day (`key_invalid`, D124, unchanged). A 403 stops the day and leaves the key's status as it is (D124, unchanged).
- **A 401 or 403 on our own key stops the day too** (review of PR 505). On `LAP_API_KEY`, a 401 stops the day as `key_invalid` and a 403 as `key_forbidden`, after that one call. Before, each member failed alone until the cap of 120. Nothing is marked: `LAP_API_KEY` has no stored status. A stopped day leaves its waiting members waiting with no later turn, and the next quota day starts a new queue.
- **New members are added on the next tick.** A member who joins during the day is not in the day's roster. Every answer carries the tracker's roster of the player's alliance (`alliance_members`) and the time the tracker last updated that alliance (`alliance.updated_at`). When the answer's alliance is the one being read and the tracker updated it after the day's roster read started, its uids are queued, due now. A member already in the queue keeps their state. Only the uids are parsed: no other field of that roster is read, stored or logged. The next batch reads the newcomer.
- **The KvK reserve stays, as a wait.** On `LAP_API_KEY`, every call still leaves `squadsReserve` (D117: the margin of 100, plus the event's reads on the event's quota day). A call that would cut into it now ends the batch with the row still `running` and `reason: quota`. It is no longer a skip of the whole day. Each later tick checks again, and the queue goes on once the event is over and its reserve is free. D117's start rule (`min(members, 120) + reserve` before the first call) is gone, and the day is skipped only when its quota is unknown or its key cannot read.
- **Event windows on our key.** On `LAP_API_KEY`, no batch runs from the scheduled event's Before until its After's window closes (`inEventWindow`), samples included (INVARIANT `squads.event-pause`). A workspace key funds none of our event reads, so it is not paused (D124).
- **Friday and Saturday** get no special window any more: the reserve handles them. #474's Friday 23:30 ST burst and Saturday 10:00 ST read will reuse this queue's call and storage; they are not built here.
- **The cap stays** (INVARIANT `squads.calls-capped`): at most `SQUAD_CALLS_MAX` (120) calls a day, retries included; past it the day stops with `reason: cap`.
- **No `fresh`.** No `/quota` call on `LAP_API_KEY`. On a workspace key there is one `/quota` call at the day's start, as in D124.
- **The press and the backup path.** /admin's "Run the squads read now" (D117, amendment 2026-11-19b, #482) and `POST /api/squads-read` run the same batches on the same queue, so a press reads only what the ticks have not. A press still refuses during the event window (`event_window`), near the quota reset (`quota_reset`), before the day's read (`daily_read_pending`) and with no target (`no_target`). It runs batches while members' turns have come and another 60 s batch ends within 240 s. Its lines carry `forced: true`.
- **A failed write of a read carries no body** (review of PR 505). A database error quotes the query's parameters, here the raw body with x, y, online and avatar_url. `storePlayerRead` therefore rethrows a failure as `player_reads.write_failed <class> <Postgres code>`, with no cause, as `lib/private/http.ts` `privately` does. So `app_errors` and `console.error` never hold a body field.
- **Logs.** `squads.batch` keeps D117's counts and adds `joined` (members queued from a newer roster) and `left` (members still waiting). There is no uid, name or squad in it, and no `retried` (a retry is an attempt in the queue).

**Expected calls a day.** For OGs (about 101 members): one call per member plus the retries. In the read of 2026-11-18, 5 of 100 calls answered 502, so about 106–107 calls, well under the cap of 120. A workspace key adds its one `/quota` call. On a normal night the 23:30 tick's batch reads 25 members right after the daily read, and the 23:45, 00:00 and 00:15 ticks read the rest, so the queue is done in about an hour. A slow upstream only moves the end later in the quota day. The worst case is 120 calls, the cap: about 1.2 × members.

### Every /v1/players answer is kept (issue 490)

- **`player_reads`**: one row per answer, under the alliance whose workspace read it (`union_id`), with `read_at`.
  - `raw_json` is the body less `alliance_members`. That roster is the daily roster again (D72) and most of the body: in the measurement below, the body is 53.7 KB with it and 12.2 KB without it.
  - The columns hold the facts the next features query: `shielding`, `shield_type` (read the type only when `shielding` is 1, #474), `map_updated_at` (the map scan's time, shown with every shield or squad reading, owner 2026-11-20), `last_seen_at`, `language`, `power`, `kills`, `player_union_id` (the `union_id` the body names: the player's alliance at that time), `sid`, `home_sid` and `vip`.
  - These fields are read leniently. A field that is missing or changes type is stored as null and never fails the squads read. The squads keep D117's strict schema.
- **Squads** keep each squad's `troops` (`[{id, tier, n}]`, new 2026-11-20) in `member_squads.troops`, and each hero's `honor` (its Hall of Honor level, owner 2026-11-20) in `heroes`. Neither is shown: the owner's rule (#461) is that troop counts are not shown. `honor` is left out of the "unchanged squad" comparison, so rows stored before this change do not count as changed.
- **`player_history`**: the body's `history[]` rows, one per `(union_id, uid, t)`, where `t` is the row's `observed_at` (INVARIANT `players.history-once`, `on conflict do nothing`). Overlapping reads add only the new rows. Columns: `power`, `kills`, `level`, `vip`, `player_union_id`, `union_abbr`, `sid`, `change_flags` and `nick`. A row without a readable `observed_at` is dropped. x and y are never put in a column.
  - **Deviation from the issue's key `(uid, t)`:** `union_id` is part of the key. A player read by two workspaces (one who moved from one alliance to another) keeps a copy per workspace. Neither workspace then reads, keeps or deletes the other's rows.
- **No extra call.** All of this comes from the squads read's own call.
- **The 90-day clean-up.** Once a day, after the day's first daily roster read (the same place as D125's job), `raw_json` older than 90 days (`PLAYER_RAW_DAYS`) is set to null. The columns and the history rows stay. The log line `players.raw_cleared` holds a count only.

**Privacy** (D71, D76, D22).

- Kept only for the alliance whose workspace read it, and stored by `union_id` like the squads (D117, D124).
- `raw_json` keeps x, y, online, last_online_at and avatar_url as the body sent them, as raw_json does elsewhere. No column holds them. No query here returns `raw_json`, and no log line, `run_logs` row, Discord message, AI request or analytics event carries any of the body. The shield fields are attack-relevant: they stay inside the workspace, and future readers go through `lib/private` after `requireWorkspace` (#474, #461).
- Gender, likes and skins stay inside `raw_json` and are not used.

**Export and delete** (amends D125). `player_reads` and `player_history` are in `WORKSPACE_TABLES` with scope `union`, as `player-reads.csv` and `player-history.csv`. `squad_queue` is in the list but never downloaded, like `squad_reads`. `player-reads.csv` leaves out `raw_json` (review of PR 505): about 90 days of raw bodies would pass the download's 4.4 MB limit, and they hold D71's private fields. The extracted columns and the history rows are downloaded. Deleting the workspace deletes these rows with its other union-scoped rows.

**Size** (measured 2026-11-20 on PGlite migrated from `drizzle/`, through the real write path, with a synthetic body of the measured shape: 40 history rows, 3 squads of 5 heroes, a 100-member roster; invented values).

- Body: 53.7 KB of JSON as sent, 12.2 KB less `alliance_members`. Stored as jsonb, the compressed `raw_json` averages about 2.5 KB.
- `player_reads`: about 320 KB a day for 100 members (2.24 MB after 7 days, indexes included). With raw bodies cleared after 90 days, the steady state is about 29 MB, plus about 20 KB a day of columns.
- `player_history`: 4,000 rows (690 KB) on the first day, then about 530 new rows (about 90 KB) a day when the tracker adds a row every 4–5 hours. That is about 33 MB a year.
- To be measured again on the first real day: `select pg_total_relation_size('player_reads'), pg_total_relation_size('player_history'), avg(pg_column_size(raw_json)) from player_reads;` (owner, issue 490).

**Desk and /admin.**

- /admin's Squads read section shows each workspace's quota day: "Squads today: 98 of 101 read · 3 waiting to retry · 0 not read · quota left 640", the state and the last batch. When everyone left waits for a later try, the state reads "3 waiting to try again · next try at 02:15 ST" and there is no button (a press would find nobody due). When some are due now and others wait, the state is "More to read." and a line says "Next try at 02:15 server time." Counts only (`lib/queries/squads-progress.ts`).
- Alliance settings shows the workspace's own line under the tracker key: "Squads today: 98 of 101 read · 3 waiting to try again · next try at 02:15 ST", or "Squads today: not started yet. They are read after the daily roster." (EN, PT-BR, FR). A day the tracker stopped says why, with no next try (review of PR 505): "Squads today: 40 of 101 read · stopped: the tracker refused the key", "… stopped: the limit is reached until 00:00 UTC" or "… stopped: today’s 120 reads are used" (`stopKind`, `lib/squads.ts`). /admin's state line uses the same words, "Stopped: the tracker refused the key.", and shows no "Next try" line for a stopped day. It shows only while the squads read is on (D126) and the key can read (the site's key, `ok` or `out_of_quota`). The workspace comes from the session (`lib/private/squads-today.ts`).

**Migration** `0053_squads_queue_player_reads` (`pnpm db:generate`): adds `squad_queue`, `player_reads`, `player_history` and `member_squads.troops`, and drops `squad_reads.after_uid` and `retry_uids` (the queue replaces the cursor). A `squad_reads` row left `running` by the old code gets its queue from the roster on its next batch. Members that row had already read that day can be read once more.
