## D83 — Kill share vs might share: a public section on the event page, alliance-level only, a ratio only from 3 events (2026-11-16, owner, 08zzzzk, issue #280)

Why: leaders decide titles and rewards after a KvK and want to see which alliances pulled their weight. The measure is the share of the server's kills gained divided by the share of the server's might at Start. On KvK 2026-11-14 it ran from 2.5× (NSE) to 0.07× (PSY). One event's ratio is noise: PSY's 0.07× came from a Start that did not match the battle window.

- **Public, alliance-level arithmetic.** Owner decision 2026-11-16: public, never a member name, neutral wording ("kill share vs might share", no blame words). The inputs are the alliance totals `total_power` and `total_kills` of the event's Start and End rows. No member row and no roster field is read.
- **Placement.** A section on the event page right after "War turnout" (`app/_ui/WhoCarried.tsx`), only for an event with both a Start and an End and at least one alliance with a usable figure. While the event has no End the section is not shown.
- **What a row is.** One alliance: a square mark for its might share at Start and a round mark for its kill share gained, joined by a line, on one axis from 0 to the largest share among the rows shown. Under it the two shares as text, and the alliance's share of members with kills gained from #271 when it has one. The marks and the line are HTML boxes (D68); every figure is also text. The row links to the alliance's event page. The viewer's alliance (`useMyAlliance`) is highlighted and always shown.
- **Arithmetic** (`carriedShares`, `lib/compute.ts`). Shares are taken over the alliances with a usable Start and End and all three totals present; an alliance whose Start or End row is stale or failed has no figure and is not in the denominators (INVARIANT freshness.no-stale-delta). A fall in an alliance's kills total is a gain of 0. When the set has no might or no kills gained there is no section.
- **Order and no bottom list** (adopted adversarial review). Rows come biggest gap first (kill share minus might share). The page shows the first 5 rows whose kill share is above their might share, and the viewer's own. (Superseded by the 2026-11-16 amendment below.) It never shows a "worst" list: there is no "Show all", because an expanded list sorted by the gap ends with the alliances whose kill share is lowest. Defenders and support players gain no kills; the section says so under its title. The kingdom's total (alliances counted, kills gained, might at Start) is one line above the rows.
- **Ratio only from 3 events, with its range** (adopted adversarial review; `CARRIED_MIN_EVENTS = 3`). An alliance's ratio is shown only when it has a ratio in at least 3 events, counting this one: the up to 12 earlier events of `chainEvent` (real events and manual rounds public in history) before this one, whether or not they have samples, plus this one. Then the row shows the ratio and its lowest and highest ratio over those events (`1.8×–2.9× over 4 events`). With fewer events the row shows the two shares and the gap in percentage points (`+6.1 pts`), and no ratio. The earlier events are read from the alliance totals by `lib/queries/carried.ts`, one query for the event ids and one for their rows.
- **Amendment 2026-11-16 (owner): "Show all (n)".** The owner overrides the no-bottom-list rule above. The default view stays (first 5 rows above their might share, the viewer's own, the kingdom's total line); a "Show all (n)" button (`ShowAllButton`, as in WhoFought) opens every alliance with a figure, in the same order (biggest gap first). Wording stays neutral: no "worst" or "bottom"; the defenders and support note stays. The line "the alliances whose kill share is furthest above..." is hidden once the list is open, because it no longer describes the rows.
- **Not done.** A ratio for an event in progress, weighting by members, a link from the Discord picture (#276).
- **Amendment 2026-11-17 (owner delegated; persona panel + adversarial review): points on every row.** Every row's headline figure is the gap in points (`+6.1 pts`), the unit the order uses. The ratio is never shown as a figure: from `CARRIED_MIN_EVENTS` (3) events it appears only as its range on the second line (`1.8×–2.9× over 4 events`). Reason: one list mixed two units (ratio and points) while rows were ordered by the points gap, and a single-event ratio such as 0.07× is noise that reads as shaming. This replaces "the row shows the ratio and its range / with fewer events the gap in points" above. `CarriedRow.ratio` is removed; `spread` stays.
- **Amendment 2026-11-17 (copy pass, panel batch D): the rows line is gone.** The line under the totals, "The alliances whose kill share is furthest above their might share, and yours." (`ledger.carriedShown`), was removed as a repeat: the rows and "Show all (n)" already show which alliances are listed. The help under the title now reads "Each alliance's share of the kills, next to its share of the might. Kills from Start to End, might at Start. Defenders and support players gain no kills." The defenders and support note stays, as the 2026-11-16 amendment requires.

- **Amendment 2026-11-21 (owner; D69 amendment "kills over the members").** Kills gained are those of the members in both the Start and End rosters, a fall counting as none, as #271's `gained` (`stayedGains` in `lib/queries/carried.ts`, also for the earlier events' ratios). A member who joined mid-event no longer brings their lifetime kills into the share. The might stays the alliance total at Start. The help under the title now reads "Each alliance's share of the kills, next to its share of the might. Kills its members in both lists gained from Start to End, might at Start. Defenders and support players gain no kills."
