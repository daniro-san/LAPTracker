## D122 — A responsive layout system: 360 / 768 / 1024 / 1440, the ledger spread, master-detail, a rail and a sidebar on the desk, shared layout components (2026-11-19, owner, 11ap, issue 412; amends DESIGN.md "Layout")

Why: the owner's critique of the whole app (2026-11-17, issue 412). Every page is one column capped at 960 px, so tablets and laptops waste their sides and every page is long; it reads as generic AI design. The owner also asked (comment, 2026-11-17) that the pass update the design system as a whole: shared components used by the desk and the public pages, no one-off page styles.

**Audit (11ap, local production build, seed data with invented names, light, EN).**

- Every page's content is at most 960 px wide at 1024 and 1440 (`Frame`, `EventView`, the alliance and player pages each repeat `mx-auto max-w-[960px]`). At 1440, 480 px of the width is empty.
- The desk shows its five bottom tabs at every width, centred in a 640 px strip at the bottom of a 1440 screen.
- Page heights did not shrink above 768. Members: 2,378 px at 768, 1,024 and 1,440. Public alliance page: 7,497 (360), 4,052 (768), 3,857 (1024 and 1440). Public ledger: 6,068, 2,990, 2,797, 2,797.
- What stacks without need: Members has six rows of controls before the first member and a list with two numbers across 960 px; the alliance page stacks summary, leadership, biggest changes, members, standing and across events in one column; Calendar puts a full month grid above the week's day cards; Home stacks four cards of very different weight.
- No layout component exists in `lib/brand/components`. Tailwind's `md`, `lg`, `xl` prefixes are used 1, 4 and 0 times; `sm` 289 times.

**Research** (read 2026-11-19).

- Window classes and canonical layouts: compact < 600, medium 600–839, expanded 840–1199, large ≥ 1200; list-detail shows both panes only when expanded; a supporting pane takes 30 % beside 70 % when expanded and goes below the main content when compact — Android adaptive layouts, https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive/use-window-size-classes and https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive/canonical-layouts, https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive/list-detail.
- Navigation per width: a bottom bar when compact, a rail otherwise ("easier to reach"), a permanent drawer as an override for expanded — https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive/build-adaptive-navigation; rail 80 dp, 3–7 destinations — https://github.com/material-components/material-components-android/blob/master/docs/components/NavigationRail.md. Apple: a sidebar needs a lot of space, shows at most two levels, is not hidden by default; keep five tabs or fewer — https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars.md, https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars.md.
- Grid: Carbon's 2x grid, 4 / 8 / 16 columns with 16–24 px margins and the grid capped at 1584 — https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/2x-grid/overview/. Margins of 16 compact and 24 above (Material, through https://github.com/hamen/material-3-skill/blob/master/skills/material-3/references/layout-and-responsive.md; the m3.material.io pages did not open).
- Tables: freeze the head row, compare in tables, cards on small screens — https://nngroup.com/articles/data-tables, https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-tables/; row heights 40–48 for touch — https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/.
- Measure: 50–75 characters — https://baymard.com/blog/line-length-readability; at most 80 — https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/visual-presentation.html.
- Tabs and accordions: tabs for grouped content not needed at once, few tabs; on desktop show the sections rather than collapse them — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/, https://www.nngroup.com/articles/accordions-on-desktop/.
- Template sameness: "the tell is the absence of decisions"; one dominant element, spacing as a language, the task sets the layout — https://www.saasui.design/blog/saas-ui-looks-ai-generated (a vendor blog, used as a design opinion, not research).
- Community tools: Discord designs mobile as its own experience rather than a squeezed desktop; members are a tap away on mobile and a side column on desktop — https://discord.com/blog/improving-our-mobile-experience.

**Decision.**

- **Widths:** 360 (design base), 640 (cards to table, unchanged), 768, 1024, 1440. Tailwind `md` 768, `lg` 1024, and `xl` redefined to 1440 (unused today). Margins 16 / 24 / 24 / 32, gutters 16 / 16 / 24 / 24, content at most 1200 px, prose 65ch, forms 560 px.
- **Desk navigation:** bottom tabs below 768; an 88 px rail (icon over word) from 768 to 1439; a 224 px labelled sidebar from 1440 that also lists each tab's views and the members' board. Same five destinations everywhere. The public pages keep their header.
- **The ledger spread** from 1024: a main column (2/3) for the page's one dominant thing, in drawn cards, and a margin column (1/3) on the right for what is read beside it, as unframed margin notes with ruled heads, divided by a 2 px ink rule, sticky when shorter. HTML order is the phone order; the margin column is never on the left. This is the brand's answer to "generic": a ledger page with its margin, not a sidebar and a grid of equal cards.
- **Master-detail** from 1024 for Members, Follow-up and Former members (list 7/12, the open item 5/12, sticky, in the URL as `?m=`); the sheet below 1024.
- **Tables** grow by column priority (640, 1024, 1440), head row sticky from 1024. Rows stay ≥ 44 px at every width: no density mode, because tablets and touch laptops get the wide layouts.
- **Page head** with actions on the right from 768; one primary action plus "More ▾" below 768.
- **Charts (D68)** take their column's width, never the page's; 160 px tall from 1024; numbers beside them in the margin column; HTML bars keep at most 24 px each.
- **Components** in `lib/brand/components/layout/`: `AppShell`, `NavRail`, `NavSidebar`, `PageHead`, `MoreMenu`, `Spread`, `MarginNote`, `CardGrid`, `MasterDetail`, `ResponsiveTable`, `Measure` (DESIGN.md "Layout components"). Pages compose them and add no frame-level grid, max-width or breakpoint classes of their own.
- **Screenshots** for every page a session touches: 360 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 in both modes (was 360 / 390 / 1280).
- **Page by page, side by side at ≥ 1024:** desk Home (today and tomorrow, to review | members this week, recent activity); Members (list | member); Calendar (the week's days | the month, add an event, the clock note); Notices (the list as a card grid; the editor | the preview and the language states); Follow-up (list | member); Roll call (the marks in two columns from 768, read down each column | the counts and "Mark the other n", sticky); Garrison (who needs help | enemy might and the spare squads); members' board (the week strip and events | notices, Start here, the thank-you note); public ledger (the ledger | status, legend, next update, my alliance; movers under it, alliances and members side by side); alliance page (members | figures, leadership, biggest changes; across events | standing over time); player page (across events and daily progress | identity, figures, names history).

**Persona panel** (2026-11-19, low-fidelity mocks of desk Home, Members and the public alliance page at 360, 768, 1024 and 1440; two rounds).

- Round 1: all four accepted with changes. Main objections: the margin column changed sides between pages (designer); boxed cards everywhere still looked like a template (designer); six rows of controls before the first member on a phone (R4); the Members split weighted toward the detail, no marked open row (R5); the sidebar squeezed content at 1024 and 768 still one column (member on a tablet).
- Resolved in round 2: margin column right everywhere with HTML in phone order; unframed margin notes with ruled heads and the pen rule; one primary action plus More on phones; 7/5 split, marked open row, sticky detail, extra columns only from 1440; rail until 1439 and the sidebar from 1440; summary and leadership side by side at 768.
- Round 2: the R4 accepted; the designer, the R5 and the member accepted with small changes, now rules in DESIGN.md ("Nothing short wraps", "Even rhythm in the margin column"): changes on one line, leadership two-up only at 200 px cells, 32 px above margin heads, actions on their own row on the desk from 1024 to 1279, chips in one scrolling row.
- Still open: Calendar and Notices were not mocked, and the rail's two-line labels were not seen in PT and FR. Each is checked by the persona panel in its own rollout PR before merging.

**Not done.** No density switch, no collapsible sidebar, no column chooser for tables, no drag to resize panes: each can be added when a page needs it. The public pages get no side navigation. Nothing changes below 768 except the "More ▾" menu and the nowrap rules.
