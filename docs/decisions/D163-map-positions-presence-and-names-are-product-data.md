## D163 — Map positions, presence and names are product data (2026-11-28, owner, branch feat/d163-map-and-names; amends D22, D71, D76, D142 and D71's amendment of 2026-11-27, issue 791)

Why: the owner, in chat on 2026-11-28: "para com esse baguio de privacidade do mapa e dos nomes, isso ai já passou, se a api serve esses dados a gente pode usar" (stop the privacy rules on the map and the names; if the API serves the data, we can use it). On public pages the owner added the same day: "na pagina publica nao vamo mostrar se o jogador 'ta online ou nao" (public pages do not show whether a player is online).

1. **On the officers' desk** (`app/(private)/w/[slug]` and `app/(private)/api/w/[slug]`, through `lib/private`, after `requireWorkspace`, for the workspace's own alliance), the roster fields the upstream API serves may be read, stored, shown and exported: map positions (`x`, `y`), `online`, `last_online_at`, `last_seen_at`, `joined_at`, and member names. A name may be shown next to its map position. This replaces D71's and D76's "never in copies or exports" for those fields on the desk, and the 2026-11-27 amendment of D71 (issue 791) that the officers' map carries no uid and no name.
2. **Public pages never show whether a player is online**: `online`, `last_online_at` and `last_seen_at` stay off every public page, route and image. Other public-page uses of `x`, `y`, `joined_at` and names are not decided here; until the owner decides, public pages stay as they are (D76, D89).
3. **Kept (log hygiene)**: no player data in logs, `run_logs`, analytics or PostHog (events, properties, session replay: per-member values stay inside `ph-no-capture`), AI or translation requests, Discord messages beyond what D159 already allows, or a shared cache. `app_errors.detail` and feedback text keep passing through `redactPrivate` (D115, D157). `lib/queries/blocked-keys.ts` keeps blocking these keys for public read models, logs and the redactor. Secrets rules are unchanged. `avatar_url` is still never returned or logged; the photo reaches pages only as the image `/img/avatar/[uid]` serves (D22). Fixtures keep the fields neutralised and names invented.
4. **Built with this decision**: the officers' map (Insights → KvK) names each dot's member in a readout under the plot (a tap on a dot or the 44 px ‹ › steps, in name order; D158 rule 7 (a)), and each near/mid/far row lists its members by name behind a tap. Event samples (D69) store each member's `x` and `y` as `alliance_snapshots.member_x` and `member_y`, parallel to `member_uids`, from the reads the sample already makes (no new upstream call). Like a Before or After row's `raw_json`, they are stored for every sampled alliance; only the own alliance's rows are read on the desk.

- **Amends D22, D71, D76, D142**: their bans on these fields on the desk give way to point 1; their public-page and log rules stand except as point 2 tightens them.
- **Amends D71's amendment of 2026-11-27 (issue 791)**: the map's view carries names (point 4).
- **Amendment 2026-11-28 (issue 821): last seen and joined on the member card, and a not-seen reason.** The desk member card shows "Last seen in game" and "In the alliance since" from the newest end-of-day roster's `last_online_at` and `joined_at` (`lib/private/members.ts`, `lib/private/member-card.ts`), and "no data" when the roster sent none. The Details tab no longer repeats them. `player_reads.last_seen_at` is not used: one source, the roster. Follow-up's "Worth a hello" group gains one reason, "not seen in game since <date>", for a member whose `last_online_at` is 3 days or more before the newest read; the date is the evidence. A member with no `last_online_at` is never listed, a member on a break has no reasons (D75), and a member still in D127's grace for new members is not listed (they stay New). The reason is in the shared reasons list, so the Members tab's "Check in" status and the card's status lines use it too. No colour, no count, no automatic action. Own alliance only, through `lib/private` after `requireWorkspace`; nothing goes to public pages, logs, analytics or AI, and the ready message reuses its quiet line.
- **Amendment 2026-11-28 (issue 817 part 2): other tracked alliances as clusters on the officers' map.** Owner, 2026-11-28 in chat: data the API serves may be used; TAG·count clusters from the owner's own reference screenshots on #817. The other tracked alliances are shown on the officers' map as muted clusters labelled "TAG · n". `lib/repo/map-clusters.ts` reads, from the stored daily rosters of the newest server day on or before that KvK's day (no upstream call), each other alliance's tag, member count and the mean of its members' `x` and `y`, only for an alliance with at least 3 placed members (`MIN_PLACED`), so a mean is never one member's own place. The mean is computed in the database, so no other alliance's member place, name or uid leaves it or reaches the page. Desk only (Insights → KvK, through `lib/private`, after `requireWorkspace`). Per-member places stay own-alliance only (point 1); nothing goes to public pages, logs, analytics or AI.
- **Amendment 2026-11-28 (owner, issues 818 and 822): public per-member alliance fields.** Public pages may show per member rank, quest points, Alliance Help coins, tech donated and might; point 2's "other public-page uses … stay as they are" gives way for those fields.
