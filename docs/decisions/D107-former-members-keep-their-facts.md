## D107 — Former members: who left stays findable with their officer facts, only public numbers beside them (2026-11-17, owner, 11x; amends D80)

Why: member facts are append-only and never deleted (D80), but the Members tab showed only the newest roster, so a member who left or was removed vanished with everything officers recorded. An officer asked: "I wanna keep information on who we had".

- **Who is a former member.** A uid that an earlier roster of the workspace's own alliance listed (a daily read's member arrays, D60 and D72; a public event sample's arrays, D69; or a public event Start or End roster's member rows in `member_snapshots`, which keep no arrays) and that the alliance's newest roster of any kind (the one the Members tab shows) does not list. Rosters of other alliances are never read (D71), so a player who moved is a former member of the old alliance, seen there up to the move.
- **The view.** The Members tab has two links at the top, "Members" and "Former members" (`?view=former`), drawn as chips with the open one pressed. The former view lists them newest leavers first (the last roster that listed them), 25 at a time, with a name search (accents and case do not matter). Each row: the name, "Last seen <day>" (the server day of that roster), "Might <v> · Kills <v>" from that roster, and the facts recorded, on one or two lines. A row opens a sheet with the facts, "Edit" and "Paste what they told you".
- **Facts stay editable** (amends D80's "a uid gets facts only while it is in the alliance's newest roster"). `saveFacts` now accepts a uid that is in the newest roster, was in any earlier roster of this alliance, or is an open pending member of this workspace (D106). Officers can add why someone left in Notes. Only facts are widened: roll call marks and every other write keep `isMember` (the newest roster).
- **A member who comes back** has the same uid, so the Members tab shows their facts again as soon as a roster lists them; nothing is copied or restored.
- **Privacy (CLAUDE.md).** The former-member read (`lib/repo/workspace-past-roster.ts`) selects only uid, name, might and kills, from the roster arrays and from `member_snapshots`, and never touches `raw_json`. A former member's map position, online status, last online, join date, donations, honor, activity and photo URL are never read, returned or logged; the list shows no photo. A test stores those keys in `raw_json` and checks that the answer holds only the name, might, kills, last-seen day and facts.
- **Not members any more.** Former members are left out of roll calls, caravan and Elixir suggestions, the list's counts and filters, the thanks and the alliance's numbers: those read the newest roster, as before. Wording stays neutral (D75): "Former members", "Last seen"; no reason or judgement is shown unless an officer wrote one.
- **Cost.** The view reads every stored roster of the alliance once per open, unbounded for now: about 100 uids per daily read plus each event's Start, End and samples, roughly 365 × 100 rows a year from daily reads alone. It is read only when the view is open, never for the members list. Bound it by date, or keep a last-seen table, if it shows up in timings.
