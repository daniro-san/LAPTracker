## D154 — A member tells their officers their own facts: AD Tech, time zone and languages from Me, kept apart from the officers' facts until an officer puts them on the card (2026-11-22, owner, issue 301; amends D80, D105, D142)

Why: officers ask each member for their Alliance Duel tech, time zone and languages (D80), wait, translate the answer and type it in. The member is the one person who knows these facts. The owner, 2026-11-22: "301 is very interesting, validate it and think this feature through properly and carefully with the personas, and build it." Starting point: the triage's narrow version on #301. Plan and panel: `.specs/features/301-member-self-report/plan.md`.

**Who sends what.**

- A member whose "This is me" link an officer approved (D142) sends, from their own page (`/w/[slug]/board/me`, "Tell your officers"), three of their own facts: Alliance Duel tech (0–100 %), time zone (an IANA zone; "Use this phone's time zone" fills the browser's) and the languages they read (D80's 18 codes). Each takes D80's own values and bounds.
- Never: main squad might (the tracker reads it, D117), troops, status, away notes, notes, any free text, any private roster field (x, y, online, last_online_at, joined_at, daily_*, active, avatar_url), or anything about another member.
- The uid is always the approved link's (`meAccess`, INVARIANT `self-report.own-uid`). The body is strict and names no uid: a body with `uid` is 400.
- An officer's own approved link (D87) lets them send for their own player the same way; the card says it is self-reported.

**Kept apart from the officers' facts** (rejected: a member's value written into `member_facts` with its own source, which `latestFacts` would make the newest value at once, silently over an officer's and into every screen that reads facts).

- A new table, `member_self_reports` (migration `0070_member_self_reports`): one row per workspace user and field, the newest send only. `version` counts that user's sends of the field; a send names the version the page showed (D80), so a stale tab is refused with 409 and the stored state. `seen_version`, `seen_by`, `seen_at` record the send an officer last answered. A null value is a removal ("You took back what you sent").
- Keyed by the workspace user, not the player: the officers see only the reports of the user who holds the approved link to that player now and is not blocked. A removed or pending link, an impostor whose link was removed, a blocked user and a member who moved to another player all disappear from the card and Home; the same user's reports show again when their link is approved again.
- `factsOf` never reads the table. Nothing a member sends changes a status, a reason, Follow-up, a roll call, a caravan suggestion, Raid prep, Insights, the thanks or "Still to ask" until an officer puts it on the card (D75).
- `member_facts.source` may be `member` ("from the member"), written only by "Put on the card" (the check constraint is replaced by a wider one; no row changes). The swap is a plain drop and add, as `0041_squads.sql` did: `scripts/migrate.ts` runs each migration in one transaction, so `NOT VALID` then `VALIDATE` would still hold the add's lock through the scan. A widened `in (…)` list cannot fail on existing rows; the scan is tens of milliseconds warm at D149's one-year scale.

**What the member sees.** "Tell your officers" opens the main column on Me, with: what the three facts are for and that the officers decide what goes on the card; that only what they send here shows under each field ("Your officers may already know it: send what is new or changed."); how to take something back (empty it and send, which does not change the card by itself). Each field is filled with their own last send, never the officers' value. Under each: "You sent this on Sun, Nov 22 · not seen yet", "… · an officer has seen it", or "You took back what you sent, on … ". Never whether it was used, never the card's value (D142). Send stays at the bottom of the screen while the form is on it, with "Not sent yet: 1 change" once something changed (first-glance test: a picked zone looked finished). Languages show in their own words and the reader's ("Português (Portuguese)").

**What officers see.** On the member card's header, while a report is new, a tinted block "Sent by the member": per field, its name with "New", "Member says: 72%" (or "Member took back what they sent"), "Self-reported · Sun, Nov 22", and "On the card: 70%" or "On the card: nothing yet" (or "Same as on the card."). Two answers, 44 px each:

- **Put on the card** writes the member's value as the fact's next version, source `member`, only while the fact is still at the version the card showed (`saveMemberFact`). Any officer's save since is refused with 409, languages included, and never combined as D80 combines an officer's stale edit: a combined list is a value nobody saw. The refusal holds at the write too: a save landing between the read and the write takes the version first, the unique version lets only one in, and the report stays new (review of PR 684). The report is then seen. A removal cannot be put on the card.
- **Seen, keep the card** marks it seen and changes nothing on the card. Under it: "“Seen, keep the card” leaves the card as it is."

Each answer names the user, the field and the version the card showed: a report that changed since, or whose writer no longer holds the approved link, answers 404 and the card is read again. Once seen, a report that differs from the card's value is said once more under that fact on the Info tab: "Member says: 74% · Sun, Nov 22 · not on the card". Home's "To review" lists "‹player› sent their own info: Time zone, Languages", linked to the card, for members in the newest roster only. It is not counted in the Home tab's number: that number is read on every desk page, and the count would need the roster there too.

**Routes.**

- `POST /api/w/[slug]/me/facts {requestId, changes: [{field, version, value | null}]}` (1–3 changes, fields unique, strict): `requireBoard`, the `board` feature, an approved link (`403 not_linked` otherwise), the member in the newest roster (`409 left`), a request id (D100: a request id a field still holds answers the stored state and stores and counts nothing; one overwritten since meets versions that moved on, so its fields are refused), at most 20 sends a day per workspace user (`auth_attempts` key `day:selfreport:<user>`, counted from the first send, `429 too_many`; a send that stores nothing is not counted; the key is deleted with the workspace, D125). A value the same as the stored one is no new send and stays seen, so a member cannot make it "New" again. Excluded from the officer-route walk in `member-session.test.ts`, with that reason; tested in its own `route.test.ts`.
- `POST /api/w/[slug]/members/[uid]/self-report {action: "use", user, field, version, factVersion} | {action: "seen", user, field, version}`: officers of this workspace only (a member's session and another workspace's get 401, the walker's fixture). Both routes run through `privately`, so an error reaches `app_errors` as a code alone.

**Privacy.** No private roster field is written or shown anywhere new; a member reads nothing about another person or about the officers' records beyond "an officer has seen it" for their own send. Nothing is logged, posted to Discord, sent to AI or translation, or put in a shared cache. The form and the card's lines are `ph-no-capture`. The workspace download (D125) gains `member-self-reports.csv` (without the request id) and the deletion removes the rows.

**Analytics** (`docs/analytics/tracking-plan.md`): browser `ws_self_report_sent` `{change: "save" | "remove"}`; `ws_action` `self_report` / `accept` (Put on the card) or `mark` (Seen, keep the card). No value, field or member.

**Cost.** Me: one more read (the member's own rows, at most 3). The card: one more read (the player's reports joined to the link). Home: one more read (the unseen reports, bounded by users × 3). The table never grows past one row per user and field (D149).

**Persona panel (2026-11-22, invented data).** Plan review: six officers (UK, PH, FR, US, UA, CH) and a data-honesty reviewer; three members (new, long-standing, EN as a second language) and product strategy; a privacy and security reviewer and an adversarial reviewer. All approved with changes; applied: EN, PT-BR and FR for every string; "Put on the card" for "Use this"; labelled values ("Member says", "On the card") in a block set apart; the member's lead saying officers decide; "Alliance Duel tech (AD Tech), in %"; "You took back what you sent" instead of "Removed"; members who left out of Home; the go / no-go measure; a stale fact version refused for languages too; a same-value send stays seen; `privately`; the counter purged with the workspace. First-glance tests at 360×780: round 1, members 4/5 (a hurried member never pressed Send) and officers 6/6, 5/6, 4/6 ("Mark seen" read as approve; "—" read as loading). After "Not sent yet" with Send kept in view, "Seen, keep the card" and "On the card: nothing yet": round 2, members 5/5 and officers 6/6 on every question, no confident wrong answer.

**Go / no-go** (product persona): four weeks after release, at least 30 % of members with an approved link have sent once, at least 70 % of reports are answered within 7 days (`seen_at − written_at`, database, read-only), and "Still to ask" falls for linked members. Otherwise revisit before adding fields.

**Not built (follow-ups):** troops and an away note from the member (an away note changes roll calls by itself and needs its own decision); "Put all on the card"; a notification to officers; a per-alliance switch (it lives under the board's, D126); Send off until something changed; room under the sticky Send bar; "Still to ask" and Follow-up saying a report is waiting.
