## D90 — Alliance page: standing against the server over time, a rank list with a small trend per row (2026-11-16, owner, 08zzzzp, issue #274)

Why: alliances compare themselves with the server. The daily reads (D60) give every alliance's might per day, and nothing showed it over time. A 20-line chart was illegible at 360 px (persona panel), so the figure is a ranked list.

- **Where.** A section "Standing over time" on the alliance page (`/events/[id]/alliances/[unionId]`), after the members and before Across events. Public, alliance totals only: no member row, name or roster key is read.
- **One read, shared with #283.** `dailyAlliances` (`lib/queries/balance.ts`) is the balance page's read of every usable daily roster in the last 90 days (D85); `powerBalance` and `allianceStanding` both use it. No second query.
- **Which days.** Only days on which every listed alliance has a usable daily roster (`dailyFit`: fresh, no error, not empty) enter a rank series, as the balance trend does. A stale or failed row gives no figure for that day (freshness.no-stale-delta). The listed alliances are those with a usable read in the last 90 days.
- **Period.** The newest complete day, and the complete days in the 7 days before it. The first of them is the base of every change. No From/To choice yet; with fewer than two such days the section says there are not enough reads.
- **The figures** (`lib/standing.ts`, pure). Rank by might per day, 1 is the greatest; equal might is ordered by union id, so the ranks are always a permutation of 1..n. Rank move: places gained since the first day (up is positive). Growth: (newest might − first might) / first might, in percent; none when the first might is 0. "The server" is the sum of the listed alliances' might, said as "the tracked alliances' total" (as D85); its growth is the growth of that sum, which lies between the least and the greatest alliance growth.
- **Wording.** One sentence first: "[OGs] OldGrandmas is #4 by might, up 2 since Nov 9. Its might changed +3.1% since then. The tracked alliances' total changed +2.4%." The sentence names the actual first day, not "7 days", because a missing day moves the base. Neutral: ranks and arithmetic, no verdict, no colour for growth.
- **Layout.** A list, a "Tracked alliances" row first, then one row per alliance by rank today: rank and move (`#4 ▲2`), badge and name, might, growth since the first day, and a sparkline of its rank. Below 640 px the row is a card of two lines with the sparkline at the right; from 640 px one line. The page's own alliance has a plum bar and tint and a visible "This alliance" chip; the viewer's alliance (`useMyAlliance`) is tinted yarn with a "My alliance" chip. The list shows the first 5 rows, the page's and the viewer's alliance wherever they rank, and "Show all (n)" for the rest (`app/_ui/StandingList.tsx`, the rule of `visibleEntries` in `app/_ui/WhoFought.tsx`).
- **The sparkline** (`app/_ui/charts/Sparkline.tsx`, D68): hand-built SVG, the line and a dot on the newest day, rank 1 at the top, at least 4 places of range so a one-place move is small. No `<text>`; its `aria-label` gives the first and last rank with dates. The ranks of every day are under Details, one row per alliance.
- **Not done.** A From/To choice (D68 Interaction: URL state, `.drawn-field` selects), a longer period, kills or members standing, event rosters in the figures.
- **Amendment 2026-11-17 (owner delegated; persona panel + adversarial review).** (1) The first sentence names both days and both ranks and no move words: "[OGs] OldGrandmas was #4 by might on Nov 16 and #6 on Nov 9." Plural forms of "up N places" break in languages with several plural forms (D48), and "since {from}" shifted with the period. The row's `#4 ▲2` stays. Keys `up`, `down`, `held` and the `moveText` helper are gone. (2) The period is the window of the 7 days up to the newest day with any usable daily read (8 calendar days, inclusive). The listed alliances are those with a usable read in the window; the period is the days of the window on which every listed alliance has one; fewer than two such days shows "Not enough daily reads yet". Before, one alliance without reads made every later day incomplete and the section showed a period that ended up to 90 days ago. (3) Alliances read in the 90 days but not in the window are named under the list ("Not ranked, no usable daily read in this period: …"). The "Tracked alliances" total and growth cover the listed alliances only.
- **Amendment 2026-11-21 (issue 473, persona review).** The first sentence is in time order: "By might, [OGs] OldGrandmas went from #6 on Nov 9 to #4 on Nov 16." Still no move words. Each row's growth since the first day carries ▲ or ▼ and the yarn or seal colour, like every change (DESIGN.md three channels). The tag is joined to the name by a no-break space, so `[OGs]` never ends a line alone. The viewer's own alliance row is tinted yarn at 5 % (was 15 %), so seal text on it keeps 4.5:1 in dark.
