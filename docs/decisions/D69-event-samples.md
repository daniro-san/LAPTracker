## D69 — Event samples: a reading every 15 minutes between an event's Start and End (2026-11-16, owner, 08zzzza, issue #279)

Why: the owner wants charts of the busiest moments of an event (heatmaps, who fought when) to plan who joins when. An event has only a Before (Start) and an After (End), so nothing shows when anything happened. Spike #183 (Option B, 08zzh) designs samples, windows and schedules in one build that rewrites every Start/End reader; that is too large before the KvK of Saturday 2026-11-21. This is build 1a: samples for single-window events, with Start and End unchanged, so data starts being collected now.

- **Shape.** A sample is a `snapshots` row with `phase = 'sample'` and `slot_at` (the slot instant, UTC; null on a Before or an After). Its alliance rows are `alliance_snapshots` rows with the members as D60's parallel arrays (`member_uids`, `member_names`, `member_power`, `member_kills`; a null power or kills stays null, D15). No `member_snapshots` rows and `raw_json` null, so a sample never stores a blocked roster key. An error row has no arrays. Migration 0018. Measured on the review (08zzh): ≈ 63 KB per sample of 20 alliances with names.
- **Constraint.** D1's guarantee is kept: `snapshots_event_phase_unique` becomes a partial unique index on `(event_id, phase) where phase <> 'sample'` (one Before and one After per event, as before), and `snapshots_event_slot_unique` is a partial unique index on `(event_id, slot_at) where phase = 'sample'`. A check ties `slot_at` to the phase. The insert of a sample is its claim (`on conflict do nothing`), so two ticks in one slot cannot both take it (INVARIANT `sample.once-per-slot`). Why two partial indexes and not one `unique nulls not distinct (event_id, phase, slot_at)`: each rule reads as its own guarantee, the Before/After rule keeps its name and meaning, and the slot index is already the shape the window build needs (`(window, slot_at)`). The cost: an insert's `on conflict` must name the index's predicate (`lib/repo/snapshots.ts`).
- **Slots and the tick.** `SAMPLE_EVERY_MIN` (env, default 15, 0 = off, must divide 60) sets a grid from the hour: :00, :15, :30, :45. A slot is due from its instant until the next one. An event (scheduled or rehearsal, never a manual round, INVARIANT `tick.skips-manual`) is sampled at a slot when the slot is strictly after its `before_at` slot and strictly before its `after_at`, its Before has finished (any status but `running`), and it has no After row. The tick's order is unchanged with one step added: create the scheduled row, then a due phase (Before and After always win), then a due sample, then the Friday check, rehearsals and the daily read (D21's one action per tick; a tick that runs a phase never samples). The tick passes the slot that holds now, so a missed slot is never caught up. Sampling stops once the After has a row.
- **Collector.** `runSample` reads the event's alliance list (the one the Before stored, INVARIANT `event.after-uses-event-copy`) through the same `/v1` path as a phase: `?fresh=1` (D64), the 502/503/504 retries, `MAX_UPSTREAM_CALLS`, a 429 stops it; a 12 s timeout per call (`SAMPLE_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS`) and a 25 s run deadline (`SAMPLE_DEADLINE_MS`) in place of the adapter's 30 s and D25's 100 s (see the Saturday hardening amendment below). No ranks board and no internal fallback (D5 covers Before and After): a failed alliance gets an error row, as the daily read does. A stale row is stored with `is_fresh = false`, which makes the sample `partial`. A sample writes `run_logs` under its snapshot id, so `/admin/runs` lists it as its own run ("Sample 10:15") and links the same run page. A sample does not update `player_frames` (D66); the Before and After do.
- **Budget** (INVARIANT `budget.samples-yield-to-after`). Before a sample, `remaining ≥ sampleQuota(n) = requiredQuota(n) + 1 + 4n`: the After's reserve plus the sample's worst case (its quota call and every read retried 3 times), 131 for 20 alliances. Below that the sample is skipped: its row closes `failed` with `quota: remaining … < required …` and a `quota` log line. After any sample at least `requiredQuota(n)` remains, which is what the After needs to start, so the middle samples always give way first. `dailyReadQuota` is unchanged: since D72 the daily read runs every day at 23:30 server (01:30 UTC the next day), so it draws on the next UTC day's quota, not on the event's; a rehearsal's one or two samples cost about 21–42 calls on its day.
- **Readers.** Every read of an event's Before and After filters samples out with one predicate, `phaseSnapshot` in `lib/queries/visibility.ts` (INVARIANT `queries.phases-not-samples`): the event page and `GET /api/events*` (`listEvents`, `getEventDetail`, so compare D57, the event picture D58 and the public Discord message too), alliance pages and alliance history, names, ledger ranks history, the upstream banner, member history (`eventUsable`: a sample has no member rows and would read as a 0-member card), the admin target and rehearsal panels, the rehearsal verdict, `scheduledEventDone` and the event's admin message blocks (`listPhaseReports`). Readers that join `member_snapshots` (player pages, search, movers, rosters) never see a sample. Only `eventSamples` and the admin run lists read samples.
- **Read model.** `eventSamples(db, eventId)` (`lib/queries/samples.ts`): a public event's readings in time order, Start (at `before_at`), the samples (at their slots), End (at `after_at`); per alliance the read time, the fresh flag, might, kills, members, and the roster as `{uid, might, kills}`. A reading still running or with no usable alliance, and a failed alliance row, are left out. No page uses it yet.
- **Discord.** No public message for a sample. On the admin channel the first sample of an event that ends `partial` or `failed` posts one message (the event's name, the sample's run block, a link to `/admin/runs`); later samples of that event that fall short post nothing (INVARIANT `notify.sample-once-per-event`), so an outage is one message per event, not one per sample. The event's own message (D31) is not edited by a sample. Skipped rather than edited because an edit does not notify (D31) and the run list already holds each sample.
- **Why Start and End stay as they are.** Every existing reader (37 files on dev quote a phase) keeps its output with one filter each, and the Before and After keep their member rows, `raw_json`, ranks board and internal fallback. The rewrite of those readers is the window build's work.
- **What the window build changes.** An `event_windows` table (event, start, end, cadence); a sample then belongs to a window, and `(window_id, slot_at)` replaces `(event_id, slot_at)` (every sample already knows its event and slot, so the backfill is one window per event). A window's first and last samples become its Start and End, so Origin of Chaos (two windows, next on 2026-12-05) gets a Start and End per window; readers move from the `before`/`after` phases to the windows; the budget rule reserves one After per window end still due that UTC day; the admin message gets one edit per window end. Schedules from `/admin` and the charts are later builds (charts on D68's SVG components).

- **Amended 2026-11-16 (review).** The not-a-sample predicate is defined once, as `phaseSnapshot` in `lib/repo/snapshots.ts`; `lib/queries/visibility.ts` re-exports it, so one grep for `phaseSnapshot` finds every reader of the Before and After, in `lib/queries` and in `lib/repo` (`scheduledEventDone`). A row already in memory is tested with `isSample(phase)`, next to it (`loadVerdictInput`, `listPhaseReports`, `eventSamples`). `eventRanks` filters with `phaseSnapshot` in SQL, so a sampled event no longer loads its sample rows on the event page, compare or alliance-in-event pages.

- **Amended 2026-11-16 (Saturday hardening).** Measured on 2026-11-16: a live `?fresh=1` read takes about 1.5 s, 3 of 18 reads in a 48-minute probe hung until the client timeout, and AFR and URSA answered 502 repeatedly. cron-job.org abandons the tick at 30 s and notifies after 2 failures (D30), and the tick awaits the whole run, so a sample must end before 30 s. (1) `SAMPLE_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS = 12_000` is each call's timeout in a sample (the quota call too); a Before and After keep the adapter's 30 s (`CallOptions` on `V1Source`). `SAMPLE_DEADLINE_MS = 25_000` is a sample's run deadline in place of D25's 100 s: alliances not read by then are named `not fetched: deadline` in the run's summary, as in a phase, and the sample closes `partial`. `SAMPLE_NOTIFY_CAP_MS = 28_000` cuts the one admin message when the response would pass 28 s. (2) An alliance with an error row in any earlier sample of the same event is read once, with no retries, in the later samples of that event (INVARIANT `sample.failed-reads-once`; `failedSampleUnionIds`, one query over the event's sample rows), so a persistent 502 does not spend its retries and the deadline in every sample. A Before or After's failure does not count. `sampleQuota` is unchanged: the deadline and the no-retry rule only lower a sample's worst case, never raise it. Release rules: (a) release migration 0018 only at a day and time when no Before, After or manual round can be claimed: the previous build's `onConflictDoNothing({ target: [eventId, phase] })` cannot match the new partial index, so between the build's migrate step and the new deployment taking traffic, or after a rollback, a claim would fail; (b) never roll back production below migration 0018.

- **Amended 2026-11-21 (wrong-alliance reads; owner).** In event 263 the upstream answered four `/v1` reads of alliance X with another alliance's roster under X's own `union_id`: another tag, another name and other members. The four rows shared 0 members with X's Start row and its newest daily roster; every good sample row shares 93 % or more. The rows were stored as X's, and the server pulse showed ±27M spikes. Start and End were not affected. The orchestrator deleted the four rows on 2026-11-21, with the owner's approval. Two alliances renamed in November, so a tag that differs is not by itself a wrong read.
  - **At collection** (`checkIdentity` in `lib/collector-identity.ts`; INVARIANT `collector.identity-checked`). A read for X is a failed read of X (`IdentityError`; row error `v1 wrong alliance (id)` or `(tag)`) when:
    - its `union_id` is not X; or
    - its tag and its name both differ from X's known labels (the board's in a Before and an After, and `allianceLabels`; a null label matches nothing), and it shares less than `SAME_ROSTER_MIN` (20 %) of the smaller list with X's newest daily roster (`lastMemberUids`, read once per run).
  - **Renames.** A rename keeps its members and passes, and the row is stored under the new tag. With no known label, or no daily roster yet, a read passes.
  - **The daily read too** (`lib/daily-read.ts`, D60): the same check, so a wrong body never becomes the next check's reference roster. It stores an error row for that alliance.
  - **Logging.** The read's `v1.fetch` line carries the code and http 200. Neither the line nor the row names the read's tag or members.
  - **Quota.** The check runs after the call has answered, so it adds no `/v1` call and no retry.
  - **Failure path.** A rejection takes the existing failure path:
    - a sample stores an error row, which `sample.failed-reads-once` counts;
    - a Before or an After makes D5's one internal fallback read, which is checked the same way. A wrong `/v1` body is a `/v1` failure, so D5's use is not widened.
  - **Not done at read time.** A sample row stores the read's tag, but a tag alone cannot tell a wrong read from a rename. Members could tell, but the pulse's reader returns no members (issue 307). The read-time guard was therefore dropped in review.

- **Amended 2026-11-21 (kills over the members; owner).** In event 263, one alliance showed +6.10M kills. +5.34M of that was the lifetime kills of 3 members who joined mid-event, so the event's own kills were +758K. Each figure now uses one of two bases.
  - **Ledger row and alliance page.** The figure stays the alliance total, End minus Start.
    - When members who joined or left moved Might or Kills, the `*` (`legend.members`) becomes tappable: a `<details>` with a 44 px target (`app/_ui/SplitMark.tsx`, copy `split.*`).
    - It shows "Total", "Came in with {n} who joined", "Went with {n} who left" and "Without them". "Without them" is the total, less what the joiners came in with, plus what the leavers took.
    - Joiners come in with their figures at their first reading of the event in the alliance (a sample, else the End), for Might and Kills alike, so what they gained or lost after joining stays in. Leavers take their Start figures. A null figure counts as 0, as #271 (`rosterChanges`, `lib/queries/rosters.ts`, summed in SQL).
    - Event 263, the alliance above: kills +6,100,900 total, +5,342,582 came in, +758,318 without them; might −140,397,584 total, +95,453,796 came in, −235,851,380 without them.
    - The legend item reads "Includes member changes: tap * for the split" (`legend.membersTap`) when a mark opens. Each mark's screen-reader name names the figure and the alliance. A tap outside the panel or Escape closes it.
  - **Derived views, compared over the members.**
    - "At the last reading" standings: each member's kills less their kills at their first reading of the event in the alliance (`Reading.gained`). Event 263: +729,772 for the alliance above.
    - Server pulse and fight clock (D86): the members in both consecutive readings (`Reading.step`), so a joiner, a leaver or a member missing from one row neither spikes nor dips a slot.
    - Who carried and kill share vs might share (D83): the members in both the Start and End rosters, as #271's `gained`.
    - The help lines say so (`ledger.liveStandingsHelp`, `ledger.livePulseHelp`, `ledger.carriedHelp`).
    - `memberGains` (`lib/queries/live.ts`) does this in SQL. Two numbers per row leave the database. Measured on event 263: 66 ms per cache miss.
