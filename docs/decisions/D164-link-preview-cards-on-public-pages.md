## D164 — Link preview cards on public pages (2026-11-28, orchestrator, issue 830, branch feat/830-link-previews; amends D22's amendment of 2026-11-14 and D65's link preview)

Why: issue 830 (roadmap v3, adopted by the persona panel and the adversarial review, 2026-11-28): a pasted link to an alliance, player or event page should show its numbers in Discord and other chat apps.

1. **The card.** `/players/[uid]`, `/events/[id]`, `/events/[id]/alliances/[unionId]` and `/alliances/[unionId]/history` set `og:image` and `twitter:image` to a 1200 × 630 PNG drawn by `next/og` (`lib/link-card.tsx`), with `twitter:card summary_large_image`, and set `og:title` / `og:description` (and the Twitter ones) to the page's title and the same facts as one line (`app/_ui/preview.ts` `cardPreview`). This replaces the small square preview of D22's 2026-11-14 amendment (the badge) and of D65 (the photo in its frame) on those pages.
2. **What it shows**: only what the page shows. Player: name, `[tag] alliance`, Might, Kills, Level (the summary band). Alliance in an event: `[tag] name`, the event's day, Might, Kills and Members with their change (`totalsMetrics` on the page, the ledger row's metrics on the card), and the rank by might. Alliance page: `[tag] name`, Might and Kills from the ranks board with their board rank. Event: its title (or day) and the ledger's first 5 rows (rank, tag, name, Might, Might's change); its text names the first 3. Never online, last seen or any other blocked roster key, and no game art: no badge, no photo, no frame. Brand only: the plum band with `MarkLine` and the site name, Alegreya / Alegreya Sans / Source Sans 3 from `lib/brand/fonts`, the `YarnCorner` ornament, tokens from `lib/brand/palette`. Text goes through `drawable` (`lib/event-image-parts.tsx`), so the renderer never fetches a font or emoji. No URL on the picture.
3. **Where it reads.** Routes `/img/card/player/[uid]`, `/img/card/event/[id]`, `/img/card/event/[id]/alliance/[unionId]` and `/img/card/alliance/[unionId]`, from the public shared cache (D143) only: the player page's `player` entry (`sharedPlayer`), the event page's entry (`loadEventPage`; the alliance-in-an-event card reads its row there too), and a new `alliance-card` entry (newest tag and name, ranks board row). No upstream call. An unknown or malformed id is the bodyless 404 of `/img`.
4. **Language and cache.** One picture serves every viewer, so it is drawn in English ("en" numbers and labels); the og text follows the page's language. `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300, s-maxage=300, stale-while-revalidate=3600`, as long as the public JSON routes: the reads behind it change with every run. A card URL with any query string (even a bare `?`) gets a 308 to the same URL without it, before anything is read or drawn, so a made-up `?v=` cannot force a render past the CDN copy. The alliance-in-an-event card's rank is the page's: the End's rank by might, else the Start's.
5. `/img/preview/player/[uid]` (D65) is no longer linked from any page. It is kept until the owner decides whether to remove it.
