## D165 — This week's raid opponent: one alliance of any server set in Raid prep, read at Saturday 00:00 ST and Sunday 00:00 ST with our own alliance on the alliance's own key, and the result on the desk (2026-11-28, owner, issue 861; amends D137, D141 and D134's estimate)

Why: the KvK opponent (D152) is a whole server, set by the owner on /admin and read on LAP_API_KEY. An alliance's raid can target one alliance on another server, and officers want to see how they did against it (owner, issue 861, 2026-11-28). A new record rather than a D152 amendment: here the officers of each workspace set the opponent, the reads follow the server day rather than an event, they spend the alliance's own key, and the result is desk-only. None of D152's rules (the /admin form, the KvK reserve, the public matchup, the End picture) applies.

**The opponent** (`raid_opponents`, migration 0092). In Raid prep (the Raid Shield roll call's page, D137), an officer sets "This week's raid opponent" for the raid of that page (its Saturday): a server number (1–9999) and a tag (D152's `opponentTag`: 1–12 characters, one path segment). One `GET /v1/alliances/{server}/{tag}?fresh=1` (the existing `fetchAllianceByTag`), sent once with no retry, checks it: 404 `not_found`, 409 `not_unique`, another `sid` `wrong_server`, our own union id `own_alliance`. The row keeps the tracker's union id, tag and name. A change or a clear keeps the old row with `cleared_at`, so Undo brings it back with no call. A raid whose End read's tick is over takes no opponent (409 `raid_over`). At most 10 checks per 15 minutes per workspace.

**The key** (D124, INVARIANT `keys.own-quota-only`). `squadsKey`'s rule: the own alliance reads on LAP_API_KEY while it has no saved key; any other workspace reads only on its own saved key, never on ours (409 `no_key`, nothing sent). On LAP_API_KEY, or a saved key that is LAP_API_KEY (issue 855), the check reads only above the site key's reserve (`siteReserveKept`, 503 `quota_low`).

**The reads** (`lib/raid-read.ts`, `raid_reads`).

- The raid is the whole Saturday, from the Friday→Saturday reset to the next one. Start: the tick right after the first reset, Saturday 00:00 ST. End: the tick right after the next, Sunday 00:00 ST; a read takes a minute or two, so at most those minutes of Sunday are in it.
- Officers move either read in 15-minute ticks from 00:00 to 02:00 ST of its day (`shield_settings.raid_start_minute`, `raid_end_minute`, default 0; Raid prep's card, `action: "times"`).
- A read's tick (15 minutes from its time) may not meet a Raid Shield read's window at the alliance's times (409 `reads_overlap`); the Raid Shield settings' save refuses times that meet the raid reads too (D137's `timesRefusal` takes them).
- Events (D160): in a week where a read's tick meets an event's window (from its Before until its After's due window closes, the tick's own rule), that week's read runs on the first tick after the window. Origin of Chaos reads 00:00–00:45 ST and is due until 01:30 ST on its Saturday, so that week's Start is at 01:30 ST. A time with no clear tick by 02:00 ST in one of the next four weeks is refused (409 `event_window`) and not offered.
- Each due tick reads, for each workspace whose Raid Shield check is on (the Raid prep switch, D126, D134) with an opponent set for that raid: the opponent (`fetchAlliance` by its union id, live) and our own alliance, one call each, no retry. Our side is the daily read's roster instead when the daily read stored it in the same tick (no call).
- The reserve is the after-reset check's (`startRead`, D137 2026-11-28b): our key from what the day's reads left, a workspace key from one /quota call on it; the morning check's calls stay (INVARIANT `budget.reserve-for-after`). Short: `quota_low`, no call.
- Each row is claimed before its call (`sent: no answer stored`; INVARIANT `raid.one-call-per-row`), so two overlapping ticks never send a read twice. A 401 or 429 on a workspace key marks it as the squads read does; a 429 stops the next call.
- **No catch-up.** A read runs only on its own tick. A tick that never came, or a read refused, failed or stale (older than 20 minutes, D152's rule), leaves that read missing; the card shows no Start→End for that side, never a number from another time (INVARIANT `freshness.no-stale-delta`).
- Stored: totals and the members as uid, might and kills arrays; no name, photo or raw body. One `raid.read` run-log line per workspace read: counts and a reason only (D71).
- The raid read runs first in the tick's `after`, before the Raid Shield reads (the owner: "the raid read goes first or right after, within the tick's budget").

**The tick's gate** (D141 amendment). `tickMayBeDue` opens on Saturday and Sunday 00:00–02:15 ST only on the ticks of a minute some workspace with an opponent set reads at (`raidMayBeDue`): "no raid read at minute m" is kept in the wake cache for every minute of the day's phase, all from one read, as #859's after-reset gate; an opponent or times save forgets it.

**The result card** (Raid prep, below the marks). "Us vs [TAG] (S328)": the Start and End lines (time, read, to come, or missing for whom), then for each side with both reads usable: kills gained, might change and members Start→End, D152's `memberChange` over the members in both reads (one who left or joined is not counted; a fall in kills counts as none), with the members counted. A side with a missing read shows only that line. No win, loss or blame words (D75): the numbers sit side by side in the same plain table. "Past raids" lists the earlier raids by opponent, newest first (up to 12). Copy in English, Brazilian Portuguese and French.

**Estimate** (D134): the Raid Shield check's tracker calls count `perWeek: 6` more: the opponent and our alliance at the Start and the End, and a /quota call before each on a workspace key, counted as if an opponent were set.

**Privacy and placement**: desk only, the workspace's own alliance; never on public pages, routes, Discord, logs or analytics (`ws_action` `raid_opponent` carries no tag, server or number). Behind the Raid Shield check's switch: off, the card is not shown and the route answers 403 `turned_off`. Rows are deleted with the workspace and are not in the data download.

**Not built**: a "winner" line; reads on other days or at other times than 00:00–02:00 ST; more than one opponent per raid.
