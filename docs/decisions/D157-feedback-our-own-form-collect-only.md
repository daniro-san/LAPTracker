## D157 — Feedback: our own form on the desk and the board, collect only, kept 180 days, read on /admin/feedback (2026-11-22, owner, issue 713 part H; amends D77, D115, D125)

Why: the owner wants officers', members' and later visitors' ideas and problems in one place (#713, 2026-11-22). The spike's Part 3 compared PostHog surveys with a form of our own; the owner approved the recommendation in the chat the same day, relayed on #713: "Feedback: the desk and the board first. Public visitors come later." "Retention: feedback is kept 180 days." "Replies: collect only, with no reply to the sender. A sender sees "Received, thank you", and patch notes credit an idea without a name." Plan: `.specs/features/713-feedback/plan.md`.

**Our own form, not PostHog surveys.** PostHog's popover and widget surveys are rejected:

- they need feature flags and a new path on our `/ingest` proxy (it forwards events, replay and remote config only), and the app runs with `disable_surveys: true`;
- they show in PostHog's own UI, not the desk's design, languages and 44 px targets;
- the desk loses a survey's "seen" state on every page load, so a popover would keep coming back;
- free text in PostHog breaks the tracking plan's rule: properties are closed lists, never free text or names (D115).

A PostHog `api` survey for a 1–5 score only stays a later option (the spike's "Later").

**Where.** "Send feedback" ends every desk page in the signed-in frame and the members' board and Me (D105, D142). Public pages come later: they need 17 languages (D48). The sheet says who reads it ("the people who make this site, not your officers"; persona check: on the board "the site owner" read as the R5). Then the kind (Idea, Problem, Something good), the message (1–1,000 characters, a hint to leave out members' map positions and online times), Send. After a send: "Received, thank you." Nothing comes back to the sender: collect only.

**Route.** `POST /api/w/[slug]/feedback {area, kind, text, path, language, website}`, strict: same-origin JSON (`isSameOriginJson`), then the area's door. `desk` takes only an officer's session of this workspace (`requireWorkspace`); `board` takes the board's (`requireBoard`, officers and members) while the board is on (`403 turned_off` otherwise). A member's session with `desk`, no session, or another workspace's session is `401 signed_out`. `path` must be this workspace's page, without a query. Excluded from the officer-route walk in `member-session.test.ts` (members send from the board), with that reason; tested in its own `route.test.ts`.

**Spam and abuse.**

- `website` is a honeypot, off screen, out of the tab order and hidden from screen readers. Filled, the route answers `{ok: true}` and stores and counts nothing.
- At most 5 a day per IP hash and 20 a day per workspace, in `auth_attempts` day counters (`day:feedback:ip:<HMAC>`, `day:feedback:ws:<id>`), counted from the first send; the IP first, so one address over its limit never uses up the workspace's. The IP is never stored: its key is an HMAC-SHA-256 under `CRON_SECRET` of `feedback-ip:<ip>` (labelled, so the key serves this use only), cut to 32 hex characters (a plain hash of an IPv4 address can be reversed by trying all of them), and day keys are pruned after a day. Past a limit: `429 too_many`, "You have sent a lot today. Try again tomorrow."

**Stored.** Table `feedback` (migration `0073_feedback`): `id`, `area` (`desk`, `board`), `kind` (`idea`, `problem`, `good`), `text`, `path`, `language` (`en`, `pt-BR`, `fr`), `workspace_id` (null only for a later public page's send), `tier` (`officer`, `member`), `created_at`, `status` (`new`, `read`, `issue`, `dropped`); an index on `created_at` for the list and the purge (D149). Never who sent it: no workspace user, uid, Discord id, name or IP. The text goes through `redactPrivate` (D115) before it is stored, so a pasted `x: 512` is kept as `x: …` and a photo URL as `…`. The redactor catches a field written with its key (`x: 512`, `last_online_at=…`, JSON); a position or an online time written as prose ("at 512,733, online 2h ago") is kept as typed, which is why the sheet asks to leave them out. So the text is a second stored free text, after `app_errors.detail` (D115), that may hold a private roster value: an exception to the private-fields rule, owner only on /admin/feedback, after `redactPrivate`, purged after 180 days, never sent to logs, analytics, AI or Discord (owner, 2026-11-22). CLAUDE.md and `.claude/rules/private.md` should name it next to `app_errors.detail`; that edit is the owner's. Without a name, the workspace, area, tier and language can still narrow a sender down in a small alliance; only the owner reads them. It never goes to a log, `run_logs`, PostHog, AI, translation or Discord.

**Kept 180 days.** The daily cleanup after the day's first read (the one that purges `app_errors` after 30 days, `lib/tick.ts`) deletes rows created more than 180 days before. A failed purge shows in /admin's "Needs you" like the others ("The 180-day feedback clean-up failed on …").

**The owner reads it** on `/admin/feedback` (PIN session, English only, D48): newest first, up to 200, filtered by kind and status (links), each with its day and time, workspace, desk or board, officer or member, language, page and text. Four buttons set the status: New, Read, Became an issue, Dropped (`POST /api/admin/feedback {id, status}`, PIN). An item becomes a GitHub issue only by hand and rewritten, because the repo is public.

**Analytics.** Only `feedback_sent {feedback_area, kind}`, from the sheet after a 200 (tracking plan, "Members' board"). The property is `feedback_area`, not `area`, which `before_send` sets on every event.

Tests: a send is stored redacted with no sender column; the honeypot stores and counts nothing; the 5th send from an IP passes and the 6th is refused, the 20th from a workspace passes and the 21st is refused; no auth_attempts key holds the IP; a cross-site request, no session, a member's session on the desk and another workspace's session store nothing; the purge deletes only rows past 180 days; /admin/feedback lists and filters; an officer's workspace session cannot set a status and the /admin pages show the PIN card without a database read; the workspace deletion removes the rows (D125's purge test).

**Amendment 2026-11-22 (owner, issue 713): one picture per send, kept by us, not PostHog's feedback tool.** The owner approved a picture for officers and members alike, and asked why we do not use PostHog's feedback tool instead of storing pictures in our database. We keep our own:

- A screenshot can show private roster fields, such as map positions and online status. CLAUDE.md forbids sending those to analytics, and PostHog keeps data for a year, past our 180-day purge.
- PostHog surveys stay off for the reasons above.
- Storage is bounded: one picture of at most 400 KB per send, at most 5 sends a day per IP and 20 per workspace, all purged after 180 days. At the limits that is at most about 8 MB a day per workspace; in practice a few sends a week.

How (D119's pattern, its code reused as is):

- **The sheet.** After the message: "Picture (optional)", the hint "One picture. Leave out screens that show members’ map positions or online times." (PT "Uma imagem. Não envie telas que mostrem posições no mapa ou horários online de membros.", FR "Une image. N’envoyez pas d’écran qui montre la position sur la carte ou les heures de connexion des membres."), and a 44 px "Add a picture" button that opens the file picker. Once chosen: a preview and "Remove the picture". The browser prepares it with D119's `preparePicture` (at most 1600 px a side and 400 KB, re-encoded as WebP or JPEG, so EXIF is dropped). The preview is inside the sheet's `ph-no-capture`, so session replay masks it.
- **The route.** The picture rides in the same JSON as `picture` (base64, at most 400 KB once decoded), so the same-origin JSON rule and the strict body still hold and a send is one request. The body is read only with a `Content-Length` (411 `length_required` without one, as D119's upload route) of at most the picture's base64 and 16 KB (413 otherwise). The server runs D119's `checkPicture` after the honeypot and before the day's counters: magic bytes (415 `wrong_type`), 400 KB (413 `too_large`), 1600 px (422 `too_big_sides`), not animated (422 `animated`); a refused picture stores and counts nothing. The sheet reads "This picture could not be used. Try another one."
- **Stored.** Table `feedback_pictures` (migration `0074_feedback_pictures`): `feedback_id` (primary key, `cascade`), `workspace_id` (`cascade`), the bytes in `bytea` (D119's column type, now exported from `lib/schema-notices.ts`), `type`, `width`, `height`, `bytes`, `hash`, with D119's check constraints and an index on `workspace_id` (D125's purge). Written with its row in one transaction. No sender, as the row. The server does not decode the pixels: a picture sent around the sheet keeps any metadata it carries, and only the owner sees it.
- **Kept 180 days**: the purge deletes the row and the picture goes with it (cascade). Deleted with the workspace (D125) and never downloaded.
- **The owner sees it** on `/admin/feedback`, under the text, as a plain `img` (next/image's optimiser would fetch it without the session) linked to the full size. It is served only by `GET /admin/feedback/picture/[id]`: the PIN session is checked before any database read; without it (an officer's or a member's workspace session included), a missing picture or a purged row, the same bare 404 (`private, no-store`). `private, no-cache` with the hash as ETag, as `/img/notice/[id]`; a matching `If-None-Match` answers 304 before the bytes are read.
- **Never** in a log, `run_logs`, PostHog, AI, translation or Discord. `feedback_sent` is unchanged: it does not say whether a send had a picture.

Tests: a picture is stored with its row and typed by its bytes; 400 KB and 1600 px pass and one byte or pixel more is refused, storing and counting nothing; a GIF and an SVG are refused as `wrong_type`; the 180-day purge removes the picture with its row; D125's purge lists the table and deletes its rows; the picture route refuses no session and an officer's session and serves the owner's; e2e: a send with a generated PNG holding an invented eXIf chunk shows the picture on /admin/feedback, re-encoded, without the chunk.
