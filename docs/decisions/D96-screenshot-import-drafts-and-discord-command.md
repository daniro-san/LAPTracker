## D96 — Screenshot import: one door in (desk upload and a Discord message command), drafts every officer reviews, the picture never kept (2026-11-16, owner, 11k, issue #299)

Why: the leadership's lists (Alliance Duel points, the Donation, Might and Kills rankings, reports and chats) exist only as game screenshots. D74 built the free OCR engine; this is the way in, the review and the save. The owner's flow, classification table and map rule are on issue #299 (comments of 2026-11-16).

- **Free OCR instead of a vision model: this supersedes #299's plan and its done_when.** Issue #299 asked for a vision model through the AI Gateway (about $0.0005–$0.01 a picture) and set done_when to "the six AD pages need at most one fix each". The owner's plan for 11k is D74's free OCR (Tesseract.js on the server, no per-picture cost, no AI Gateway). Measured on the owner's six iPhone ranking prints (923 × 2000; 41 list rows, truth typed from the pictures): rank and value right on 41 of 41 rows (before this session's preparation: 25 of 41), each picture's pinned own row recognised on 6 of 6 (before: 0 of 6); the two AD prints need no value or rank fix (8 of 8 and 7 of 7). Names are where fixes remain: on picture 98 (AD weekly), 6 of 8 names match a member for sure, 2 need a pick from the closest names; on the narrower 358 px pages 27–28, 9 of 16. The done_when is therefore met for values and ranks and not for names on narrow pictures: there an officer picks 7 names across the two pages.
- **One door in, two ways to reach it.** In the desk, "Send screenshots" takes several pictures at once. On Discord, the message command "Send to LAPTracker" (Apps on a message with pictures). There is no menu per screen: the classifier sorts.
- **Where the OCR runs: on the server** (D74's open question). The browser draws each picture on a canvas as PNG, drops a full-screen capture's status bar (the top 5.5 % of a picture at least 1.9 times taller than wide, measured on 923 × 2000 iPhone prints) and scales it to at most 1080 px wide, then sends one picture per request (`POST /api/w/[slug]/shots/read`, under Vercel's 4.5 MB body limit). Text lines come back; a second request (`POST /api/w/[slug]/shots`) turns them into drafts, classified again on the server so a map never becomes data. The Discord path does the same on the server after the response.
- **The OCR's preparation changed** (`lib/screenshot/prepare.ts`): each pixel's grey is its distance from its row's background (the most common grey over five rows, × 1.5), so white text on the green own row and coloured text on the medal rows read like dark text on light rows; JPEG, WebP and other formats are decoded by Tesseract's own image library (a no-recognition pass) and prepared like a PNG; an optional status-bar crop. A picture that is not recognised is read a second time in plain grey (no flattening): a map's labels sit on textured terrain. The committed OCR text fixtures were recorded again with the new preparation.
- **Classification scores, never guesses** (`lib/screenshot/classify.ts`). Each screen has weighted signals (header, columns, rows of the right shape, the screen's own words); a kind is accepted at a score of 3 with no tie, otherwise "not recognised". Alliance Duel's Daily tab shows its day tabs, so Daily is sure; Donation's Daily and Weekly read the same text and stay for a person to confirm with one tap. A chat page needs three or more sender lines (`Lv.NN [TAG] Name` with a message under it), so a notice with two comments is not one.
- **Kinds and saves.** Saved in this build: Alliance Duel points per member per day (Mon–Sat) and per week (`alliance_duel_points`, a member's earlier value for the same day replaced on an officer's save, the saved value shown in the draft first); the Donation, Might and Kills rankings as a check against our own end-of-day reads (D72) and the newest roster, of which only the counts are kept (`screenshot_checks`: rows, compared, same, differ). Member update (owner, 2026-11-16: every week, for every member): each chat message's squad might and AD Tech % (D80's `parseOnboarding` over the message's OCR lines) become member facts through `saveFacts`, one new version per field with the facts' source "from a message" (D80 has no "from a photo" source; a chat is a message). Each row shows the previous value beside the new one ("AD Tech: 55% → 60%"); AD Tech already at 100 % is complete, starts dropped and is never written again; a field another officer saved since the draft was opened refuses the whole save (409) with the newer value, answered per row with "Keep theirs" or "Use mine", as the facts editor does. Secret Ops report: the report never says whose it is, so the review asks for the member who was robbed (the officer's "This is me" player when set, otherwise nobody until picked) and lets the officer fix each attacker's name or drop the row; Save calls D97's `saveSecretOpsDraft` and shows how many thefts it saved and why the rest were left out, with a link to `/w/[slug]/thefts`. Recognised but "coming soon", with no draft made: damage event result, Undead Siege result and Elixir teams (#298), which add a `SaveHook` (`lib/private/import-kinds.ts`, registered in `lib/private/import-save.ts`). A hook throws `SaveRefused` to roll the save back and keep the draft open.
- **Drafts** (`screenshot_drafts`). Pictures of one kind and tab sent together merge into one draft: rows deduplicated by rank and value (or name and value), in rank order, with `gap` where a rank is missing between pages. Rows whose alliance tag is not the workspace's own are left out and counted ("turn on My Alliance"); an unread tag stays and the member match decides. Names go through D74's matcher against the newest roster and the names seen in the last 60 days: sure, doubtful (the officer picks), not found. A draft keeps each picture's OCR text lines, the rows and their matches, never the picture. Every officer of the workspace sees every draft; the Home tab shows how many wait.
- **Nothing counts before Save (D75); the first save wins.** A draft carries a version. Save, discard and change of type send the version the officer opened; the save's claim (open, that version, not expired) and its writes run in one transaction. A second officer gets "already saved by <name>". Drafts are deleted 7 days after they were made, saved or not, when the workspace's list is read or a draft is made.
- **The Discord command.** A public route, `POST /api/discord/interactions` (outside /w, in `app/(private)` so it reaches `lib/private`), is the app's Interactions Endpoint URL. It checks the Ed25519 signature over timestamp + body with `DISCORD_PUBLIC_KEY` (Node `crypto`, no dependency; not a secret) and refuses any other request with 401, as it does a signed timestamp more than 5 minutes from the server's clock (a replayed request); it answers Discord's PING. The person must pass the workspace's own rule, as at sign-in (D71): the interaction's server id gives the workspace, the member's role ids from the interaction must meet its leadership roles, or the account must be on its allowlist; anyone else gets a polite reply only they see. The route then answers within Discord's 3 s with a deferred reply only the sender sees, reads the pictures after the response (Next's `after`), and edits that reply through the interaction's webhook: "Read: Alliance Duel weekly, 8 rows, 2 doubtful → Review" with a link to the draft. The reply holds a type, counts and a link: no roster value. The officer is stored as one of the workspace's people (D73), without a sign-in time. This is the only second maker of a `WorkspaceAccess` besides `requireWorkspace`: `officerAccess` in `lib/private/access.ts`, which names the officer by their chosen display name and carries their "This is me" player.
- **Attachments.** Fetched only from `cdn.discordapp.com` and `media.discordapp.net` over https, no redirect, a 10 s timeout, at most 8 MB each and 16 per send, four fetched at a time. Reading stops 45 s after the job starts: the drafts read by then are made, the rest are counted as not read, and the reply is still edited. One Tesseract worker per function instance is reused across requests. An attachment that is not `image/png` is asked from Discord's media proxy as PNG (`proxy_url` with `format=png`) first; when that fails, the original is fetched and the reader decodes it itself. To confirm on the first real test: the proxy's `format=png` answer. The bytes stay in memory for the reading; a JPEG or WebP whose header gives more pixels than the reader accepts is refused before it is decoded.
- **Registration.** `pnpm discord:register <guild id> …` (`scripts/discord-register-command.ts`) registers the guild command with the app's client-credentials grant (`applications.commands.update`, `DISCORD_CLIENT_ID` and `DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`); no bot token. One definition (`lib/import-command.ts`) serves the script and the route. The owner can also register it from /admin: the Discord section's "Register the Discord command" (PIN, `POST /api/admin/discord-command`) runs the same `registerImportCommand` on the server, with the credentials in the Vercel env, in the server of every workspace, and shows each server's HTTP status.
- **Vercel.** `tesseract.js` is a server external package; the two routes that read pictures ship its worker, its WebAssembly core and the English data (`outputFileTracingIncludes`), and run up to 60 s.
- **Not done here.** A "Pick the type" button in the Discord reply (the desk has one; on Discord the reply says to send it from the desk); a rate limit on the import routes (officers only, a cap of 16 pictures per send); the review screens of the coming-soon kinds.
- **Names.** The sheet import (D80) took `/w/[slug]/import`, `/api/w/[slug]/import` and the `import.*` words first, so the screenshot import lives under `shots`: `/w/[slug]/shots/[id]`, `/api/w/[slug]/shots`, `/api/w/[slug]/shots/read`, `/api/w/[slug]/shots/[id]`, and the words `shots.*`.
- **Amended 2026-11-17 (D101).** `lib/import-command.ts` is now `lib/app-commands.ts`, and `registerImportCommand` is `registerAppCommands`: it registers "Send to LAPTracker" with the two Translate commands in one PUT, since the PUT replaces a guild's whole set.
- **Amended 2026-11-17 (11x, D106).** A row not found offers "New member: <read name>" beside the member picker; Save then saves the row to a pending member of that name key, made or reused in the save's transaction. A row not found also gets the three closest names under "Closest names" in its picker, as a doubtful row already did with the matcher's suggestions.
