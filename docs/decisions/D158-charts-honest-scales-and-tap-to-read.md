## D158 — Charts: honest scales, one bar with one zero rule, and four tap patterns (2026-11-22, owner, issue 736; amends D68, D83, D90, D136, D153)

Why: the chart review of 2026-11-22 (issue 736: a data-visualisation specialist, a front-end engineer, a product strategist, an officer who reads English as a second language and an adversarial reviewer on data honesty, on a local production build with the e2e seed) found scales that mislead: a zero or a loss drawn as a 6 % stub, shares drawn against the busiest row instead of their whole, a line that joins daily reads with event reads, a rank sparkline that draws a flat #1 and a flat #5 alike, and a kill-vs-might axis built from the rows on screen. None of these is a drawing problem a library would fix, so D68 stands: no chart library, labels in HTML, numbers beside every chart. The owner approved the panel's recommendations on 2026-11-22: "o que as personas recomendarem é o que vamos fazer… executar o que decidiram".

1. **One bar and one zero rule.** Every column and horizontal bar is `Bar` (`app/_ui/charts/Bar.tsx`, server-safe, no hooks). Its length is a share of its track. Null (no figure) draws nothing, not even an empty track; the NoData mark or the row's words say why. Zero or less draws a 1 px hairline. Above zero draws at least 2 px. A share above 1 is clamped to the full track. A bar never draws a fixed minimum such as 6 %. The drawing is `aria-hidden`; its words are the row's own text or an `sr-only` span beside it, never an `aria-label` on a non-interactive element.
2. **A share is drawn against its whole**, never against the busiest row at small counts: members (membership flow, time zones with a "Not recorded" row on the same scale), a cap (coins against 1,000), or 100 %. Where the game has no known cap (daily quest points, donations), the reference is the alliance's best member-day in the same 7 days; on a member's own page (D142) it stays their own best day, since an alliance figure there would show other members' values.
3. **A reference line where a threshold means something**: 100 % on KvK recovery (the scale is `max(1, highest)`, so 112 % and 120 % no longer draw alike), a cap where one is drawn against.
4. **One reading cadence per line.** A line chart joins one kind of reading (the daily reads). Readings of another cadence (an event's Start and End) are hollow dots, not joined to the line, inside the event's band, which is a light fill with dashed edges. The value range still covers every reading. Sparklines are not used: a rank change is written as text, only when the rank moved.
5. **A diverging figure is drawn from 0.** Kill share vs might share (D83) draws one bar per alliance: the gap in points, centred on 0, on one axis built from every row, so "Show all" does not move the bars. The word "points" is spelled out once, in the bar's key.
6. **Two sides of one measure sit in one row on a phone.** The KvK matchup (D152) is a table below 640 px: one row per measure, a column per server. From 640 px the two cards stay side by side.
7. **Tap patterns** (to build; issue 736 part 2). A tap opens its detail next to what was tapped, by one of four patterns: (a) inline detail, a readout under the chart with 44 px ‹ › steps where columns are too narrow to tap; (b) chart to chart, the next level in the same card with a path and a back step, the level in the URL, computed on the server; (c) a who list in place, up to 12 names inline, 13 to 60 in the sheet below 1024 px or the side pane from 1024 px, ending with "Open these n in Members ›" (`?set=<kind>:<key>`); (d) a link to a filtered page for more than 60, or when sorting, filters or actions are needed, opening with one line that says why. Choose by what the next tap answers ("what is this number?" (a); "when, or which part?" (b); "who?" (c) or (d)), then by set size. Public pages name no member they do not name today (D89). Shared rules: the label and meaning are visible at the tap, targets are 44 px, nothing is hover-only, Back keeps the scroll position, a list or child chart comes from the same function as the count that opened it, and the Details table stays.
8. **Satori.** D68's Satori limits bind the line chart only: no next/og picture draws a chart today (`lib/event-image.tsx`, `app/img/preview/player/[uid]/route.tsx`); `lib/event-image-turnout.tsx` keeps its own hex-styled bars.

- **Amends D68**: its "Not done" list and the 2026-11-16 amendment's event band (a filled `bg-ink/50` band) are replaced by rules 1, 3 and 4; its interaction paragraph is refined by rule 7.
- **Amends D83**: the square and round marks on a 0-to-largest-share axis are replaced by the gap bar of rule 5. Rows, order, figures and "Show all (n)" are unchanged.
- **Amends D90**: the per-row sparkline is removed (rule 4). A row whose rank moved says "was #6 on Nov 9" beside its `#4 ▲2`; a row whose rank held says nothing more. The ranks day by day stay under Details.
- **Amends D136**: the member card's quest points and donations are drawn against the alliance's best member-day (rule 2), not the row's own highest day.
- **Amends D153**: each time-zone bar is a share of all members, and "Not recorded yet" has a bar on the same scale (rule 2).
- **Not done here** (issue 736 part 2 and follow-ups): the tap patterns (rule 7), Members `?set=`, chart-to-chart levels, the week's pulse sentence when every day is 100 %, leaving out leading weeks with no change, and A and B written inside the balance bar's segments.

**Amendment 2026-11-22 (issue 739, orchestrator, from a read-only check of the stored reads): daily quest points have a cap.** Rule 2's "where the game has no known cap (daily quest points, donations)" no longer holds for daily quest points: the reads show a cap of 240 a day (`QUEST_DAY_CAP` in `lib/private/member-week.ts`: 240 a day (1,680 a week), provisional until the owner confirms 230 or 240). Quest points bars are drawn against it, on the member card and on a member's own page, instead of the alliance's best member-day (which, read from the running total, was a Sunday total near 1,660) or the member's own best day. A share above 1 is clamped, as rule 1 says: a day read about 30 minutes before the reset can carry a few points past it. Donations keep the alliance's best member-day (the member's own best day on their own page), and coins keep 1,000. If the owner confirms 230, only the constant and the line's number change.
