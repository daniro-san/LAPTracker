## D158 — Charts: honest scales, one bar with one zero rule, and four tap patterns (2026-11-22, owner, issue 736; amends D68, D83, D90, D136, D153)

Why: the chart review of 2026-11-22 (issue 736: a data-visualisation specialist, a front-end engineer, a product strategist, an officer who reads English as a second language and an adversarial reviewer on data honesty, on a local production build with the e2e seed) found scales that mislead: a zero or a loss drawn as a 6 % stub, shares drawn against the busiest row instead of their whole, a line that joins daily reads with event reads, a rank sparkline that draws a flat #1 and a flat #5 alike, and a kill-vs-might axis built from the rows on screen. None of these is a drawing problem a library would fix, so D68 stands: no chart library, labels in HTML, numbers beside every chart. The owner approved the panel's recommendations on 2026-11-22: "o que as personas recomendarem é o que vamos fazer… executar o que decidiram".

1. **One bar and one zero rule.** Every column and horizontal bar is `Bar` (`app/_ui/charts/Bar.tsx`, server-safe, no hooks). Its length is a share of its track. Null (no figure) draws nothing, not even an empty track; the NoData mark or the row's words say why. Zero or less draws a 1 px hairline. Above zero draws at least 2 px. A share above 1 is clamped to the full track. A bar never draws a fixed minimum such as 6 %. The drawing is `aria-hidden`; its words are the row's own text or an `sr-only` span beside it, never an `aria-label` on a non-interactive element.
2. **A share is drawn against its whole**, never against the busiest row at small counts: members (membership flow, time zones with a "Not recorded" row on the same scale), a cap (coins against 1,000), or 100 %. Where the game has no known cap (daily quest points, donations), the reference is the alliance's best member-day in the same 7 days; on a member's own page (D142) it stays their own best day, since an alliance figure there would show other members' values.
3. **A reference line where a threshold means something**: 100 % on KvK recovery (the scale is `max(1, highest)`, so 112 % and 120 % no longer draw alike), a cap where one is drawn against.
4. **One reading cadence per line.** A line chart joins one kind of reading (the daily reads). Readings of another cadence (an event's Start and End) are hollow dots, not joined to the line, inside the event's band, which is a light fill with dashed edges. The value range still covers every reading. Sparklines are not used: a rank change is written as text, only when the rank moved.
5. **A diverging figure is drawn from 0.** Kill share vs might share (D83) draws one bar per alliance: the gap in points, centred on 0, on one axis built from every row, so "Show all" does not move the bars. The word "points" is spelled out once, in the bar's key.
6. **Two sides of one measure sit in one row on a phone.** The KvK matchup (D152) is a table below 640 px: one row per measure, a column per server. From 640 px the two cards stay side by side.
7. **Tap patterns** (to build; issue 736 part 2). A tap opens its detail next to what was tapped, by one of four patterns: (a) inline detail, a readout under the chart with 44 px ‹ › steps where columns are too narrow to tap; (b) chart to chart, the next level in the same card with a path and a back step, the level in the URL, computed on the server; (c) a who list in place, up to 12 names inline, 13 to 60 in the sheet below 1024 px or the side pane from 1024 px, ending with "Open these n in Members ›" (`?set=<kind>:<key>`); (d) a link to a filtered page for more than 60, or when sorting, filters or actions are needed, opening with one line that says why. Choose by what the next tap answers ("what is this number?" (a); "when, or which part?" (b); "who?" (c) or (d)), then by set size. Public pages name no member they do not name today (D89). Shared rules: the label and meaning are visible at the tap, targets are 44 px, nothing is hover-only, Back keeps the scroll position, a list or child chart comes from the same function as the count that opened it, and the Details table stays.
8. **Satori.** D68's Satori limits bind the line chart only: no next/og picture draws a chart today (`lib/event-image.tsx`, `app/img/preview/player/[uid]/route.tsx`); `lib/event-image-turnout.tsx` keeps its own hex-styled bars.

- **Amends D68**: its "Not done" list and the 2026-11-16 amendment's event band (a filled `bg-ink/50` band) are replaced by rules 1, 3 and 4; its interaction paragraph is refined by rule 7.
- **Amends D83**: the square and round marks on a 0-to-largest-share axis are replaced by the gap bar of rule 5. Rows, order, figures and "Show all (n)" are unchanged.
- **Amends D90**: the per-row sparkline is removed (rule 4). A row whose rank moved says "was #6 on Nov 9" beside its `#4 ▲2`; a row whose rank held says nothing more. The ranks day by day stay under Details.
- **Amends D136**: the member card's quest points and donations are drawn against the alliance's best member-day (rule 2), not the row's own highest day.
- **Amends D153**: each time-zone bar is a share of all members, and "Not recorded yet" has a bar on the same scale (rule 2).
- **Not done here** (issue 736 part 2 and follow-ups): the tap patterns (rule 7), Members `?set=`, chart-to-chart levels, the week's pulse sentence when every day is 100 %, leaving out leading weeks with no change, and A and B written inside the balance bar's segments.

**Amendment 2026-11-22 (issue 739, orchestrator, from a read-only check of the stored reads): daily quest points have a cap.** Rule 2's "where the game has no known cap (daily quest points, donations)" no longer holds for daily quest points: the reads show a cap of 240 a day (`QUEST_DAY_CAP` in `lib/private/member-week.ts`: 240 a day (1,680 a week), provisional until the owner confirms 230 or 240). Quest points bars are drawn against it, on the member card, on a member's own page and in Insights' effort groups (which drew against the highest group), instead of the alliance's best member-day (which, read from the running total, was a Sunday total near 1,660) or the member's own best day. A share above 1 is clamped, as rule 1 says: a day read about 30 minutes before the reset can carry a few points past it. Donations keep the alliance's best member-day (the member's own best day on their own page), and coins keep 1,000. If the owner confirms 230, only the constant and the line's number change.

**Amendment 2026-11-23 (issue 736 part 3): rule 7's pattern (b), chart to chart, is built.** Four levels, each drawn with `Bar` in its chart's place under a path (`DrillPath`, `app/_ui/charts/DrillPath.tsx`) with a 44 px Back step; every link keeps the scroll position, and the level is in the address query, so a link reopens it. (1) The server pulse on an event page: the readout's "11:00–11:15 by alliance ›" opens that interval's kills by alliance (`?interval=<i>`). (2) The fight clock: the same split for one event's slot, in its strip's place (`?event=<id>&at=<minute>`). Both come from `serverPulse`'s own loop (`PulseSlot.parts`), so the alliances add up to the interval; the split names the alliances it leaves out (not read fresh in both readings), draws an alliance that lost kills as 0 in its bars and shares (so they are shares of the gains, never past 100 %) and names it, and says on the fight clock when their sum is a loss counted as 0. A split names its two real reading times (the first interval starts at the off-grid Start). (3) Insights' week's pulse: an earlier week's row opens its seven days for each measure (`?pulse=<monday>`, `pulseDays` beside `pulseOf`); the line under each says the days add up to the week, except the middle donation, whose line says each day shows its own median and medians do not add up. (4) Insights' membership flow: "Day by day ›" under a week with a join or a leave opens its days (`?flow=<monday>`, `flowDays` from the week's own moves), counts only (D75), with the line that they add up and why a day shows "—" (no daily update, or before the first comparison). On the desk the path sticks to the top of its card. Not built: a flow day to its came-from and went-to bars, a pulse day to who reached it, and a KvK to its recovery days.

**Amendment 2026-11-23 (issue 752): the day levels, and two flow fixes.** Built as pattern (b) was, each in its chart's place under `DrillPath` with a 44 px Back step to the level above, the level in the address query. (1) A membership-flow day with a join or a leave (`?flowDay=`, from a day row of the week level) shows its came-from and went-to bars, counts only (D75), drawn against the members; they come from the window's own sightings (`cameFrom` and `wentTo` over that day's moves), so they add up to the day's joined and left, and the day's count is written above them. Back goes to the week's days, this week too. (2) A pulse day (`?pulseDay=`) shows, for daily quest points, the coin cap and a donation, "n of N members" with the names of those who did it behind a tap (pattern (c): in place, in name order, nobody else named, no Members link), and the day's middle donation as a value with no names. `reachedOn` gives each read's sets and `pulseOf` counts with it, so a day's names are its column's count. This amends D139's "Still counts only" for the pulse (see D139's amendment of this day): issue 736's chart review, which the owner approved on 2026-11-22 ("o que as personas recomendarem é o que vamos fazer"), lists a pulse day's "who reached it". An officer can still tell who is not named; no view lists or counts those members by name, and with one donor the middle donation is that member's: both are accepted here as officer-only (the persona panel, 2026-11-23). (3) A KvK recovery day (`?kvk=<KvK day>&kvkDay=<day>`) shows the Start, End and that day's might of the counted members its read lists, the share back its column shows, and "n of the N members this KvK counts" with how many that read leaves out; a day whose read lists none of them has no link and opens nothing (its column is "—"). The KvK alone (`?kvk=`) shows its recovery days unfolded, the level Back returns to. The 7-column grids of the pulse and of the recovery get a row of 44 px day links under them, as a column is about 40 px wide at 360 px. Under a week or day path the flow card shows only that level; its whole-period parts return with Back. Fixes: a flow week none of whose days can show a change (no daily update, or only the first one) shows "—" and "nothing to compare", never 0; the flow card's based line names the daily update its weeks start from ("in these weeks, since …"), the one the first change is compared with, and the starting-point line on its days the same date; with fewer daily updates than the page reads (36), that is the alliance's first one, the date the tenure line gives, and with more, the tenure line keeps the alliance's first daily update and each line says which start it gives.
