## D113 — Notices: "Shorten to 1,000", Translate localizes within the board's room, and review requests between officers (2026-11-17, 11ad, issue #402; amends D81)

Why: a notice over the game board's 1,000 characters was cut when copied, and translations often came back longer than the source. The owner asked for a way to shorten a text without losing its key points, for Translate to write as a native officer would within the room, and for a light way to ask another officer to check a language (issue 402; owner comment of 2026-11-17: approve, or edit and save; no comment thread).

**Shorten to 1,000** ("Fit" in the code: `fitText`, `fitSystem` in `lib/ai/notice-writing.ts`; `kind: "fit"` on `POST /api/w/[slug]/notices/draft`).

- The button shows on any language tab whose text, trimmed, is over `NOTICE_LIMIT` (counted with `noticeLength`, graphemes), and is then the main action (`button-primary`): first, across the row on a phone, first of three from 640 px. Its words: "Shorten to 1,000 / Keeps the key points. You can undo." ("Reduzir para 1.000", "Réduire à 1 000"); the limit and the counter use the officer's thousands format. Hidden otherwise; the route refuses a trimmed text within the limit with 400. The line under an over-long text reads "555 characters over the game’s 1,000 limit. The game may cut the end." (persona panel, 2026-11-17).
- The answer is a draft ("Draft from Shorten to 1,000, not saved") with Accept and Undo, like Improve writing. It is one Groq request through the same route and models, so it uses the same free-tier quota (D81); there is no separate counter.
- The prompt keeps every date, day, time, name, number, place and call to action, cuts repetition, greetings and filler first, adds nothing, and keeps D81's term rules and D75's people-first line. It writes in the tab's language in that language's officer voice (below), so Fit on a translated tab uses that language's localizer voice.
- Budgets: the first request asks for 95 % of the limit (950). If the answer is still over 1,000, one more request asks for 80 % (800). If that is over too, the route answers 422 `still_over` and the editor shows "Could not shorten this text to the limit. Your text is unchanged: shorten it by hand, or try again." The officer's text is not touched. Worst case is four model calls (two tries, each with its fallback), inside the route's 90 s.
- Logs: `notices.fit.ok` (ms, retried), `notices.fit.still_over` (ms). Never the text.

**Translate localizes** (`localizeSystem(to, limit)`).

- The notices' Translate now sends a system prompt per target language: "You localize alliance notices into …", with a voice line for each language (Brazilian Portuguese as a Brazilian officer writes it to the members, "vocês", never European Portuguese; French as a French-speaking officer writes it, « vous »; English plainly). Rules added before D81's: localize, do not translate word for word; keep every key point; keep the register; game terms as the alliance uses them (the game's own word where `TRANSLATED_TERMS` gives one, D52); no machine-translated or AI-sounding phrasing, and no greetings, sign-offs, emoji, headings or filler the text does not have; stay within 1,000 characters, and say the same key points in fewer words if a full translation would not fit.
- D81's rules stay as they were: the `<text>` block and its neutralising (D101), the data-only line, the glossary, the do-not-translate list, the weekday and heading rules, the worked example, the "mostly untranslated" retry. The request still carries only the officer's text.
- Discord's "Translate for me" (D101) keeps `TRANSLATE_SYSTEM` unchanged: `translateText` uses the localizer only when given a limit, which only the notices route passes.
- **A fitted translation stays linked to its source** (coordinator, 2026-11-17). Fit on a translation made in this editor keeps its "translated from" link, so the save takes that source's basis and does not mark the other languages out of date, and Translate from that tab still refills it from the source. Fit on hand-written text behaves like Improve writing: the text becomes the officer's own. Undo of that Fit brings the translation back still linked to its source; Undo of a Fit on a translation not yet accepted goes back past the translation, and the link goes too.
- A translation that still comes back over the limit is shown as it is, with the counter in red and Fit beside it. Translate does not call Fit by itself.

**Review requests** (`lib/private/notice-reviews.ts`, `POST /api/w/[slug]/notices/reviews`, migration `0038_notice_reviews`).

- On a notice's page, "Ask for a review" opens a sheet: what (the whole notice, or one written language) and who (anyone on the team, or one officer). The officers listed are the workspace's users with an officer session on record who are not blocked, without the one asking.
- `notice_reviews` keeps each request (notice, language or null, reviewer or null for the team, who asked, when) under the sheet's request id (D100). `notice_review_outcomes` keeps how it ended ("approved" or "edited", who, when), one per request, under its own request id. Neither table is ever updated.
- Who answers: the named officer; for a team request, any officer but the one who asked. Others get 403 `not_reviewer`; a second answer gets 409 `closed`; a repeat of the same answer's request id gets 200.
- The request shows at the top of the notice with the language by its own name: "Review asked by Ana · Français" (or "· the whole notice"), then "For you · …", "For Rook · …" or "For the team · …", and "Jump to Français". Whoever may answer gets "Approve Français" and "Edit Français and save"; the latter opens the editor at that language with the line "Reviewing. When you save, the request closes as edited by you."; the save carries `review: {id, requestId}`, and the "edited" outcome is written in the save's own transaction (`writeNotice`'s `after`). A save refused with 409 writes no outcome. A save from a request that is not the officer's, or already closed, still saves and leaves the request as it was. The language's card shows a "Review waiting" tag, and each written language's card has its own "Ask for a review", which opens the sheet with that language chosen. In the sheet the chosen option is tinted plum (DESIGN `radio-field`).
- The notice's "Review" section lists the five newest answered requests ("Rook approved Português · Tue, Nov 17 19:08", "Rook edited the whole notice after a review · …") with "Times are server time." once under them. A notice lists every open request; only answered ones are limited (20 read).
- Home's "To review" lists, above the screenshot drafts, every open request the officer may answer: "Ana asked you to review Français" (or "asked the team"), the notice's title on the line under it. The heading's number counts drafts and requests, and "Nothing to review." shows only when both are empty. Home's page title is now "Home" / "Início" / "Accueil" (was "Calendar, members and screenshots"; no decision quoted it).
- **The Home tab's number** (coordinator, 2026-11-17) counts these requests beside the screenshot drafts, on every page (`toReviewCount`, one SQL count with the same rule as `mayAnswer`): a request named for the viewer, or for the team when someone else asked. A request the viewer made does not count for them.
- **Recent activity** (D108): three lines from the same union read as the other small sources, with every name and title through `isolate()`. "Ana asked Rook to review the notice “X” · PT" (or "asked the team"), "Rook approved the notice “X” · PT", and "Rook edited the notice “X” after a review". A save from a request and its outcome share one instant, so the "edited after a review" line stands for the plain "saved the notice" line.
- Approval does not block Publish to Discord. Nothing is sent outside the app: no Discord message, no mail.
- The route answers 401 `signed_out` to no session or a member's session (D99, D105), and is in `member-session.test.ts`. Logs: `notices.review.asked` (whole 0/1), `notices.review.approved`.
- No comment or note field (owner, 2026-11-17).

**Amendment, 2026-11-20 (issue 412, rollout PR 4: a notice's read page in language tabs; owner: "the notices page is still bad, why isn't it in tabs").**

- A notice's read page shows one language at a time, in the editor's tab strip (`LanguageTabs.tsx`, WAI-ARIA tabs: arrow keys, Home, End, `aria-selected`, the one panel labelled by its tab). The officer's language comes first; the tab in view on opening is the first written one in that order. The stacked language cards and their headings are gone.
- Each tab shows the text's length beside its code ("PT 433", seal when over 1,000; the full "433 of 1,000 characters" for screen readers) and one state: Missing, Out of date (yarn dot), ✓ Reviewed, Translated (AI), Written, and in the editor Draft (plum dot). "Missing" replaces "Empty" in both editors (D116); PT "Faltando", FR "Manquant". "Translated (AI)": "Traduzido (IA)", "Traduit (IA)". "Reviewed": "Revisado", "Relu".
- **Translated (AI)** is stored: `notice_edits.translated` (migration `0056_notice_translated`), true when the save names Translate's source (`from`), and kept when the same text is saved again ("Keep as is"). A hand change makes it false. Notices saved before this read as Written.
- **Reviewed**: a request on that language or on the whole notice answered (approved or edited) at or after the language's last save (`reviewedLanguages`, `notice-rules.ts`). The save times stay on the server.
- In the editor a saved text keeps its saved state until it is changed; an accepted translation not saved yet reads Translated (AI); any other change Written.
- The panel holds what each language card held: "Review waiting", the text, the line over the game's limit, the three copies with their lines (D116) and "Ask for a review" of that language; a missing language says "Not written yet." and the panel takes the focus. Copies and "Ask for a review" act on the tab in view; "Publish to Discord", the calendar link and Delete act on the whole notice, as before.
- Persona panel (2026-11-20: an R4 on a phone, a product designer, product strategy; all "ship with fixes"), applied:
  - the open review requests sit under the tab strip, so the tabs are on a phone's first screen; "Jump to Français" is left out while that tab is in view;
  - one "Ask for a review", in the panel (the sheet still offers the whole notice); the "Review" section shows only when a request was answered, and lists those;
  - the "Out of date" tag in the panel is now a verb, "Update Português" ("Atualizar …", "Mettre à jour …"), with a gap above it, and opens the editor at that language; the tab keeps "Out of date". The old label "… may be out of date. Edit the notice" is gone, so the visible words are the button's name;
  - the tab's neutral state words are 12 px in the text colour.
  - Not changed: answered requests keep each language's own name ("Rook aprovou English"), as this decision set on 2026-11-17 for the request lines.
- Edit opens the editor at the tab in view, without scrolling past the title. "Jump to Français" in an open review request opens that tab.
- The tabs and the panel are at most the editor's width (`max-w-2xl`) at every width.
