## D89 — Member flow: where members went between the tracked alliances, public, counts only, moves under 5 hidden (2026-11-16, owner, 08zzzzo, issue #275)

Why: URSA emptied between 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-11 (93 members: TRI 24, OGs 21, TmS 17, CR8R 11, NSE 5, others 9, 6 left the tracked alliances), and BOH went from 67 to 31 members on 2026-11-16. Officers use the flow for recruiting.

- **Owner decision (2026-11-16).** Public, alliance-level only, never a member name, neutral wording.
- **Adversarial review (2026-11-16, binding).** A matrix cell of 1-4 members identifies people through their player pages. `FLOW_MIN = 5` (`lib/flow.ts`): no cell under 5 is shown and no destination under 5 is listed, on the table and on the phone list. The page says it ("Moves of fewer than 5 members are hidden and left out of every count"). Every figure on the page is made of the cells shown: the totals under the sentence and the order of the rows count only moves of 5 or more, and a phone row carries no member total, because the start-day total minus the cells shown would give the hidden moves (review of #349). No hidden remainder is shown either: it would give the size of a small group.
- **Page.** `/alliances/flow?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD`: a GET form of two selects (class `drawn-field`) listing the days with a usable daily read of the last 90 days (`FLOW_DAYS`), so the URL is the link to share (D57, D68). The default is the newest read and the latest read on or before 7 days earlier (`FLOW_DEFAULT_SPAN`), else the oldest read. A malformed or impossible date (2026-02-31), a day without a usable read, and a link with only one of the two days are 404s; a From that is not before the To shows "Choose a From day earlier than the To day." Linked from the alliance page's title row (`AllianceLinks`) and the merge simulator; it links back to the merge simulator.
- **How a member is placed on a day.** The alliance whose usable daily roster of that day (fresh, no error, not empty, `dailyFit`, D60) lists the uid. `daily_rosters` holds one row per alliance per day. A uid in two rosters of one day (the reads are seconds apart, so a move can land between them) goes to the roster with the later `source_updated_at`; the lower union id breaks a tie.
- **Event rosters do not count.** The daily reads exist for every server day; adding the event Before/After rosters would mix reads taken at different times of day and give a second source for the same day.
- **Which alliances are counted.** Only those with a usable daily read on both days. A tracked alliance with a read on only one of the two days is left out of rows and columns, its members count as outside the counted alliances on both days, and the page names it ("Not counted, no usable read on both days"). A member who moved into such an alliance therefore shows as "Left". This is an approximation the page states.
- **The matrix.** Rows: the counted alliances (the ones with the most moves in or out first) and "New" (in no counted alliance on the start day). Columns: the counted alliances and "Left" (in none on the end day). The diagonal is who stayed. The spec's columns "outside the 20" and "new to the 20" are read as the column "Left" and the row "New": a new member has no source alliance, so it is a source. "Changed alliance" counts members in two different counted alliances; arrivals and leavers are in the line under the sentence. Members in no counted alliance on either day are not counted.
- **First screen.** One sentence: "Between Oct 23 and Oct 30, the largest move was [NSE] Natural Selection → [OGs] OldGrandmas (6)." With no move shown: "no move of 5 or more members between the tracked alliances". Under it, counts of the cells shown in the `Label: {n}` shape (D48): "Changed alliance: 15 · New: 5 · Left: 6".
- **Layout.** From 640 px a table that scrolls inside its own `overflow-x: auto` box with a sticky first column; the number is written in each cell and the plum tint behind it is decoration (D68). Below 640 px each row is a `<details>` of at least 44 px whose open list gives that source's destinations as text with counts, then "Stayed" and "Left"; a row with no move shown is one line with its "Stayed: {n}" count. Counts are small whole numbers, so the exact-numbers toggle is not on the page.
- **Privacy.** The read selects an alliance's tag, name and each roster's uids and read time; no member name, might, kills or blocked roster key. Members stay inside `lib/queries/flow.ts` and `lib/flow.ts`; the page gets counts of at least 5.
- **Code.** `lib/flow.ts` (pure: `placeMembers`, `buildFlow`, `visibleCells`, `summarise`, `activityOrder`, with property tests), `lib/queries/flow.ts` (`flowPage`, PGlite tests), `app/_ui/FlowPicker.tsx`, `app/_ui/FlowView.tsx`, `app/(pages)/alliances/flow/page.tsx`. Words in `lib/copy-flow.ts`, translated in the 17 languages (D48).
- **Seed.** `scripts/seed-daily.ts` moves members between alliances from the fourth daily read of the second week on (6 and 5 members join the first alliance, 3 join it too and are hidden, 5 leave, 5 new ones join the fourth), so the matrix shows real shapes locally.
- **Not done.** No member names or lists, no hidden-remainder figure, no event-roster placement, no chart other than the table, no Discord message.
- **Amendment 2026-11-17 (owner delegated; persona panel + adversarial review).** (1) Stays under 5 stay hidden, and the note says "stays included": the start-day roster size minus a small stay gives the hidden remainder (the members who moved), so a shown stay would undo the hiding of a small move. (2) The totals line names its threshold ("Moves of 5 or more · Changed alliance: … · New: … · Left: …") so the figures are not read as all moves. (3) "Not counted, no usable read on both days" renders directly under the totals line, before the matrix and the phone list, because it qualifies New and Left. (4) The day pickers keep every day with a usable read: production shows no partial days (every day had all 20 alliances or none), so no marker and no threshold. (5) Layout (UX review): the totals line is full ink, each part on one line; the "Not counted" line and the hidden-counts note form one group with a left rule under it.
