## Blockers

A member on a phone hits these on 2026-11-07.

**B1. The status chip turns red whenever one alliance is stale, and reads as a warning.** `partial` maps to the seal tone (`app/_ui/Status.tsx:21`), so `Last updated: partly done at Oct 31, 14:01 server` is the only red block on the page. 7b's persona test ran four rounds with three personas; in every round all three read the red chip as an error and distrusted the 14:01 time, which is the one time the page gives for its numbers. In the seed this state is the normal one (TEST is stale in the after snapshot), and on a real Saturday one stale alliance out of 20 is likely. Evidence: `docs/screenshots/07c-detail-home-390-light.png`, `07c-blank7b-home-390-tile1.png` (the red frame with everything else blanked). Fix: the time chip in ink regardless of state, and a separate seal mark for what is partial (the `○ Stale` count, or the stale rows' own marks, which already exist). DESIGN.md `status-strip` tones change in the same commit; the owner chooses the exact shape (7b's open issue 1).

**B2. The Details panel (owner, 2026-11-05: "tables not positioned, no colour or help for reading, copy strange").** Measured at 1280 px light on the URSA card: three plain lists side by side in a `sm:grid-cols-3` (`app/_ui/EventView.tsx:28-69`), label columns of 80 px (`app/_ui/DetailLines.tsx:18`) and 56 px (`Status.tsx:146`), 14 px text, no vertical rule or alignment between the groups, numbers left-aligned after the label, no colour except the `−2 Members` note; the first two groups carry a heading (`RANK`, `BEFORE → AFTER`, `EventView.tsx:41,50`), the third none. `#4 → #4 =` is printed three times (`DetailLines.tsx:28`); `Might`, `Kills`, `Members` appear twice (ranks and values). At 390 px the same 16 lines stack. Evidence: `docs/screenshots/07c-details-1280-light.png`, `07c-details-1280-dark.png`, `07c-details-390-light.png`. Proposal for 7d: one small table per card, rows Might · Kills · Members · Server rank, columns `10:00 → 14:00 · change · #`, numerals tabular and right-aligned, notes in seal, the two snapshot times as column heads (language-free, and the same times the chip shows). The owner decides the shape; this is the one place a member finds the before and after figures, so it ranks here.
