## Findings

Ranked by how often a member meets the problem and how much of the brand it costs. High: met on every visit by a group of members. Medium: a brand rule broken where it shows. Low: drift in the guideline or one string.

### High

**H1. The product has four names and the phone header shows none of them.** The wordmark "OG's LAP Tracker" is `sr-only` below 640 px (`app/_ui/SiteHeader.tsx`, the brand link), so at 360 px the header is the seal and three controls; the name a member reads is the title band "Server 325 · Event Ledger" and the footer. At 1280 px the header says "OG's LAP Tracker" and the title band says "Server 325 · Event Ledger" on the same screen. Elsewhere: `<title>` "Sat, Oct 31 · OG's LAP Tracker", manifest name "OG's LAP Tracker" / short name "LAP Tracker", `/brand` calls the product "the Event Ledger", the Discord picture's footer reads "OG's LAP Tracker · Server 325", the two webhooks are "KvK Scout" and "LAP Tracker Admin", the README and GLOSSARY say "LAPTracker". "LAP" is expanded nowhere a member can see (GLOSSARY: Last Asylum Plague, the game). Evidence: `08zzs-brand-detail-header-360-light.png` (no name) against `08zzs-brand-detail-header-1280-light.png` and `08zzs-brand-home-1280-light.png` (two names in one view); `08zzs-brand-brand-1280-light.png` (intro: "How the Event Ledger looks"); `08zzj-after-light.png` (picture footer). Direction: pick one product name and one maker line. The ledger story in DESIGN.md Voice already gives the shape: product "Event Ledger" (or "Server 325 · Event Ledger"), maker "kept by the Old Grandmas", and "OG's LAP Tracker" only where a technical name is needed (manifest, repo), or the reverse. Then: the 360 px header carries the product name (the selector shows the date only since D49, so there is room for a short wordmark at `display-sm`), `<title>` and the picture footer use it, the webhooks are renamed to the same persona (the avatars already are one character; see Keep). Owner's call; DESIGN.md Voice and Mark change in the same commit.

**H2. The primary display face is absent in 7 of the 18 languages, and the error line is 100 % fallback in 5.** Measured on `main h1` of `/` and the 404 at 360 px (platform fonts per node): ru and uk render in Times New Roman (title: 7 glyphs Young Serif, the digits and dots; 19 fallback; the 404 line: 2 and 17), vi falls back on the stacked diacritics (title 19 / 4, 404 line 12 / 2), zh-CN in Songti SC, ja in Hiragino Mincho ProN, ko in AppleMyungjo, th in Thonburi (the 404 line in each: 0 glyphs of Young Serif). On Android and Windows the fallback is whatever serif the system has. Alegreya Sans holds Cyrillic and Vietnamese (section titles stay on brand in ru, uk, vi) and falls back for zh, ja, ko, th. `lib/brand/fonts.ts` loads `subsets: ["latin"]`; Young Serif has no Cyrillic, Vietnamese, CJK or Thai on Google Fonts at all, so this is the face, not the config. D48 records it as accepted. For the brand it means: in Russian, the language DESIGN.md names first, the page title and the one page whose "message is the page" (DESIGN.md `error-page`) are set in Times New Roman at 48 px. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-home-360-light-ru.png`, `08zzs-brand-notfound-360-light-ru.png` against `08zzs-brand-notfound-360-light.png`; `08zzs-brand-home-360-light-ja.png` (digits in Young Serif inside a Mincho title); `08zzs-brand-home-360-light-th.png`. Direction, two honest options: (a) replace Young Serif with a display serif that covers Latin, Latin-ext, Vietnamese, Cyrillic and Greek in one family with the same even, solid stroke on phones: Alegreya (the serif sibling of Alegreya Sans, same designer, OFL, 400 and 700) or Literata; three families stay three; (b) keep Young Serif for Latin and add Alegreya 400 as the display face for Cyrillic and Vietnamese by `unicode-range` (next/font `declarations` or a second family in the display stack), which breaks the "no fourth family" rule by one family nobody sees twice. CJK and Thai keep a named system serif stack (`"Hiragino Mincho ProN", "Songti SC", "Noto Serif CJK", "Noto Serif Thai", serif`) so the fallback is chosen, not left to the browser. Either way DESIGN.md Typography and D48 Fonts change in the same commit.

**H3. The header seal is a 20 px line drawing inside 40 px of wax, and in dark mode the wax is black.** `WaxedMark size={40}` draws `MarkLine` at 20 px (`lib/brand/components/Logo.tsx`), below the 32 px floor DRAWING.md sets for the hand ("below 32 px the hand disappears") and below the 96 px the Mark section sets for `MarkLine`, as the recorded exception. At 1× the mark is a scribble in a disc; at 3× it reads. In dark mode the wax tone `paper` resolves to `--paper` = `#1a1418` (`Wax.tsx` `WAX.paper = text-paper`), so the ivory seal becomes a near-black puck with a mauve scratch on the mauve bar; the brand page's own "Do: Ivory wax on the header" tile shows the same black disc in dark. This is the one brand element on every page at every width. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-detail-header-1x-360-light.png` and `-dark.png` (real size), `08zzs-brand-detail-header-360-dark.png` (3×), `08zzs-brand-brand-1280-dark.png` (the Do tile). Direction: treat wax as pigment, not as a theme token: the ivory wax stays `#efe6d3` and its ink `#3b2436` in both themes (the plum bar is `#b48ba6` in dark, so ivory on it holds), and the red wax stays `#8c2f2f` with paper ink (see M4). For the 20 px problem: the solid `Mark` cut exists for exactly this size ("the hat, beak and cowl still read at 16–24 px", `Logo.tsx`), so the header seal can press the solid cut, or the seal grows to 44 px with the mark at 28 px. DESIGN.md Mark records the exception "by the owner's choice"; this review's view is that the exception costs the mark its legibility in the one place every visitor sees it. Owner's call.

### Medium

**M1. Light-mode yarn is bronze.** `--yarn` light is `#805c12` (hue 41°, 29 % lightness) since #25 (07c S5); dark is `#e0b04a` (hue 41°, 58 %). Same hue, but on paper beside the game badges' gold the light value reads as brown: every `▲` gain on a light phone is bronze, and the Overview sentence "mustard yarn marks gains" is true only by lamplight. A mustard that clears 4.5:1 on `#efe6d3` does not exist (the lightness cap for 4.5:1 on that paper is about 32 %), so the choice is between contrast and the metaphor, and #25 chose contrast. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-detail-card-360-light.png` against `08zzs-brand-detail-card-360-dark.png`; `08zzs-brand-brand-1280-light.png` (the Yarn swatches side by side). Direction: keep the token; change the words (DESIGN.md Overview and Colors: "bronze in daylight, gold by lamplight"), and keep the yarn wax tone (`WaxTone "yarn"`, light `#805c12`) out of use, as it is today.

**M2. `○` means two things.** The stale mark is `○` in seal (legend "○ Stale", the `○ 1 stale` count, the row marks; `app/_ui/Status.tsx`), and the muted "Next update at …" chip and the "not yet" state carry `○` as their icon (`Status.tsx`, the three `icon="○"` chips). On the status strip the two sit 12 px apart: `○ 1 stale` in seal, then `○ Next update at Nov 14, 10:00`. The three-channels rule (colour, symbol, word) holds only while the colour and the word are read; in blank mode and for a colour-blind reader the symbol is the channel, and it is shared. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-detail-status-360-light.png`, `08zzs-brand-home-360-light-ru.png` (same at 360 in another language). Direction: give "scheduled / not yet" its own mark. DESIGN.md's freshness set already lists `–` for "no data"; an unfilled hourglass would say "waiting" in the same knitting-and-time vocabulary as the running hourglass. One component, one legend line.

**M3. "Back to the table."** The 404 and 500 button says "table" (`lib/copy.ts` `errors.notFound.back`, `errors.server.back`) while every other string says "ledger" (`site.home` "Ledger", the breadcrumb root, "No player by that name in the ledger", "This page isn't in the ledger"). All 17 dictionaries translated the odd word faithfully: "Назад к таблице", "表に戻る", "Voltar à tabela", "Zurück zur Tabelle". One key, 18 files plus stamps. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-notfound-360-light.png`, `08zzs-brand-notfound-360-light-ru.png`, `08zzs-brand-notfound-360-light-ja.png`. Direction: "Back to the ledger" (or the breadcrumb word, "Ledger"), then the translate-copy skill.

**M4. Dark-mode wax seals are coral stickers.** The seal buttons on `/admin` (Before, After, Rehearse, 96 px) take `text-seal`, which in dark is `#d46a6a`, a value tuned for 14 px text on dark paper (5.24:1). At 96 px it is a flat salmon disc with dark drips; the pressed ring and the lumps that make it wax at `#8c2f2f` vanish at that lightness. Same root as H3: wax tones follow the theme tokens. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-admin-360-dark.png` against `08zzs-brand-admin-360-light.png`; `08zzs-brand-brand-components-1280-dark.png` (the seal button specimen). Direction: with H3, wax is pigment: `seal` wax `#8c2f2f` with paper ink in both themes (paper `#efe6d3` on `#8c2f2f` is 6.61:1); the chip and text uses of `--seal` keep the theme value.

**M5. The guideline page and DESIGN.md Voice describe a product that has moved.** `/brand` Type scale samples "Gained and lost" (`display-md`) and "GAINED / LOST" (`label`), the Voice rule "Labels are the game's own words: Might, Kills, Members, Gained / Lost" and DESIGN.md Voice "Columns 'Might', 'Kills', 'Members', 'Gained / Lost'": no column, tab or label reads "Gained / Lost" anywhere on the site (the ledger columns are Might, Kills, Members; the movers tabs are Might ▲, Might ▼, Kills ▲, Rank ▲▼). `/brand` Movers: "Three tabs, Might ▲, Might ▼ and Kills ▲" (four since D54). `/brand` Voice lede: "Short, dry, grandmotherly, English" (the pages read in 18 languages since D48; DESIGN.md Voice already says so). Evidence: `08zzs-brand-brand-type-1280-light.png`, `08zzs-brand-brand-voice-1280-light.png`, `08zzs-brand-brand-components-1280-light.png` (Movers specimen). Direction: samples from the live copy ("Might", "Kills ▲", "MEMBERS"), the tab count from `MOVER_TABS`, and "English" → "written in English, read in 18 languages". `lib/copy-brand.ts`, `lib/copy-brand-components.ts`, DESIGN.md Voice.

**M6. The link preview is a mark with no name.** `app/opengraph-image.tsx` draws `MarkLine` at 440 px on plum and nothing else; its comment says the display serif is not available to the renderer. Since 08zzj the four TTFs are in `lib/brand/fonts/` and `lib/event-image.tsx` draws Young Serif with next/og, so the preview can carry the product name (H1) in the brand face. A Discord or WhatsApp card then shows the name twice (image and title), which is how every other product's card reads. Evidence: `scratchpad` render of `/opengraph-image` (1200 × 630, mark only; not committed, the route is unchanged since Session 7). Low cost once H1 is decided.

### Low

**L1. One emoji in the Discord copy.** `lib/copy-discord.ts` `admin.pinLocked` starts with 🔒. DESIGN.md Voice: "no emoji". The admin channel is exempt from the language-light rules, not from the voice. `⚠ ✓ ✗ ○` elsewhere are the site's own glyphs, not emoji. Direction: the dropped-stitch glyph the site uses for "failed", or the word "Locked:".

**L2. The game's badges are the loudest colour on every page.** Twenty 28 px full-saturation PNGs (red, gold, purple, blue) in a palette of paper and ink; at 1280 px they form a column of game art down the ledger, and again in Movers. D22 makes them content, the drawn frame ties them in, and this review does not propose removing them. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-home-1280-light.png`, `08zzs-brand-detail-movers-360-light.png`. Options if the owner wants them quieter: 24 px instead of 28, or the frame in `rule` tint with 2 px of paper padding, so the badge sits in a mat; never a filter (the badge is the alliance's own mark).

**L3. The settings panel's radio tiles are the only place the brand shows ten framed boxes at once.** Eleven drawn frames and a select in one 360 px panel; each frame is correct on its own (`radio-field`), and together they read as a form, not as the ledger. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-settings-360-light.png`. Not a brand break; a density question for #174.

**L4. Admin dates.** `/admin` shows `Sat, 10/31/2026, 14:01 server time` (US order) while the public pages follow the viewer's locale; 07c S3a is still open. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-admin-360-light.png`. Brand-wise the admin area now matches the site (header, lace, footer, seals, drawn frames: 07c S3c is done); the date order is for #174.

**L5. Japanese title punctuation.** The ja title is "サーバー325・イベント台帳" with the katakana middle dot `・` where every other language keeps the brand's `·`. The translator's choice and typographically right for Japanese; noted so nobody "fixes" it the other way. Evidence: `08zzs-brand-home-360-light-ja.png`.
